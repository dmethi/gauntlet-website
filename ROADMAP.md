# Roadmap

Gauntlet planning lives in the Studio team in Linear under the
[Gauntlet — Build an exceptional league and club experience](https://linear.app/dhruv-projects/initiative/gauntlet-build-an-exceptional-league-and-club-experience-d4e0af27b88e)
initiative. Reporting is shaped in
[STU-8](https://linear.app/dhruv-projects/issue/STU-8/shape-gauntlet-preview-and-weekly-recap-reporting),
and launch-quality work is shaped in
[STU-9](https://linear.app/dhruv-projects/issue/STU-9/audit-gauntlet-whole-site-quality-and-launch-readiness).

- Execution-ready and committed work belongs in Linear.
- Uncommitted possibilities live in [docs/IDEAS.md](docs/IDEAS.md).
- Durable product and architectural decisions live in `docs/`.
- Transient session context lives in `SCRATCHPAD.md` and must be pruned.

The pre-migration roadmap is preserved at
[docs/archive/roadmap-2026-07-28.md](docs/archive/roadmap-2026-07-28.md). It is
historical evidence, not an active queue.
