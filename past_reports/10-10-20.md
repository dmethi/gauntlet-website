Madurais [103.68] (2-2) vs Jeffrey [91.30] (1-3)
What a stunning come-from-behind victory on Monday night by the Madurais. Down roughly 10 going into Monday night, the Madurais were left with just Aaron Rodgers and the Chiefs defense against Jeffrey’s Matt Ryan, Clyde Edwards-Helaire, and Mason Crosby. Sleeper gave Jeffrey over a 95% chance of winning the matchup going into Monday night. Instead, the Madurais came out with the victory powered by 30 points from Aaron Rodgers and 20 points from the Chiefs defense. Absolutely unbelievable stuff. They overcame an early Austin Ekeler injury and no-shows by Higbee and Marvin Jones en route to victory. Don’t mistake this victory for signs of a good week for the Madurais, however. This was only the 4th time this season that a team that scored less than the league average for the week came out with the dub. They were due for some good luck though - this was the first time all season that their opponent scored under 115 points against them. They’ll hope to have a more commanding victory next week in their matchup against Anant in which they might be without their top 2 RBs (Henry and Ekeler).

Absolutely brutal week for Jeffrey. This was the second straight week he was labeled “worst manager” in fantasy by Sleeper, and the second straight week he had a loss decided by poor lineup decisions. In this case, he benched Joe Mixon, whose 40-point performance would certainly have helped Jeffrey beat the Madurais. In any case, Jeffrey’s lifeless team sleepwalked its way to a 91-point performance, as he didn’t have a single player score over 20 points. The worst part for Jeffrey is that he has no injuries to blame - his QB threw 39 passes, his receivers had 13 targets between them, and his running backs had almost 60 carries between the three of them. The opportunity was there. His players just suck. In an effort to inject some life into his team (or potentially trade away his talent to make his start-sit decisions easier), Jeffrey traded away his top-3 draft picks (Mixon, Clyde, and Woods) for…4 WRs and 1 RB. Someone tell this man that quantity is not greater than quality in fantasy. He’ll ride his new look team this week against Shivang’s team where he’ll face off against his top draft pick.

Ishan [127.08] (3-1) vs Hunter [124.94] (2-2)
Easily the highest scoring matchup of the week (only 1 other matchup featured two teams scoring over 100 points each), this one ended up being a nail biter as Ishan edged Hunter out by the narrowest of margins - this was the smallest margin of victory so far this year.

Ishan continued rolling this week, as he’s now one of two players who have scored over 125 points in 3 out of the 4 weeks of the season. Once again led by sensational performances from Dak and Carson, Ishan seems to have found a recipe of success - so far through 4 weeks, he’s consistently boasted strong QB and RB play. He did get a little lucky this week - his tight end only caught one ball on two targets, but that catch was for a 13-yard TD which ended up being the difference in this one. The only real weakness of his team through the first 4 weeks has been his receivers, but his output from his top two receivers has been steadily improving, up to 31.3 this week from 15 in week 1. He’ll hope to continue his sensational start to the season next week against yours truly.

Painful loss for Hunter, as this marks the second straight week he’s lost after putting up over 115 points. Despite finally receiving over 20 points from his QB for the first time all season (the Minshew-Chark connection was POPPING, as I predicted last week), Hunter came just short in this matchup. Aman [137.12] (2-2) vs Kishan [76.14] (2-2)
What a complete turnaround from last week which saw Kishan post 120 points and Aman post 60. Now cementing his status as a boom-bust player, Aman scored the highest points in the league for the second time this season. Led by standout performances from Kittle and Lamar, Aman was not slowed down at all by the loss of Davante Adams late this week and had an amazing all-around week - he had 5 players score over 15 points. His strategy of drafting two top tight ends paid dividends as Kittle and Andrews combined for over 50 points. Despite having a nonexistent RB corps at the start of the season, Aman has completely turned it around at that position as he scored well above league average this week at RB, driven by strong performances from an underrated Ronald Jones and a home run trade acquisition of Kareem Hunt. After 4 weeks, Aman has yet to score between 70 and 135 points (the rest of the league only has 5 total such performances). He’ll hope his stretch of extreme performances pushes him in the right direction this week as he takes on a struggling Akshay.

Shivang [117.70] (3-1) vs Anant [105.70] (3-1)
The ever-consistent Shivang succeeded in delivering the first loss of the season to Anant as he was able to stave off a comeback attempt from Anant on Monday night. Shivang was able to withstand Russel Wilson finally coming down to earth, as standout performances from CeeDee Lamb and Jerick McKinnon propelled Shivang to score above league average despite having ho-hum performances across the rest of his roster. Like Aman, Shivang has succeeded in completely turning around the fortunes of his RB corps and has steadily increased his RB output from week 1 to this week, and has just injected some further RB talent onto his team through his acquisition of Clyde Edwards-Helaire from Jeffrey. Now looking like one of the more well-rounded teams in the league, Shivang will hope to continue his strong start to the season next week against Jeffrey.

Varun [124.34] (2-2) vs Akshay [103.22] (0-4)
Another week, another dub for Varun as he now has scored over 120 points for three straight weeks and rebounded strongly from his 0-2 start. His lineup decisions continue to lend credence to the unique strategy of starting an inactive player, as this marks the second straight week that someone has scored over 120 points despite starting an inactive player (Kishan did this last week). How was he able to overcome this? With 35 points from OBJ and 31 points from his special teams. Despite his strong performance from this week, Varun will now have to deal with the lengthy absence of Nick Chubb from his lineup, as Chubb suffered a knee injury during the game. He’ll hope Raheem Mostert is back for next week to start at RB, because he currently has zero backup options if Mostert is out. His entire bench is literally all out for the week, and his two IR spots are filled up. He’ll hope to still emerge victorious next week and continue his hot streak as he takes on Kishan.
