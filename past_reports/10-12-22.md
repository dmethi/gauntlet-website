This has been a really fucking strange season so far. I’m not necessarily referring to Kishan finding the missing link between him and fantasy football success in dental school of all places. Nor even necessarily to the fact that Ishan has somehow turned into Mr. Comedian, dropping fire meme after fire meme with a level of consistency only rivaled by the Raiders consistency in having players run into trouble with the law.

I’m talking mostly about the football itself:

The standings - There are only 4 teams above .500, and each team has at least 2 wins and 1 loss, with the exception of the Sitarams who are still clearly reeling from their improbable and frankly hilarious collapse in last year’s playoffs.

QB value - The teams that have a top-4 QB on the year (Mahomes, Hurts, Lamar, Allen) have a combined record of 15-5, or a win percentage of .750. The rest of the league? 15-25, or a win percentage of .400. I don’t think I’ve ever seen QB drive winning to this insane degree.

WR value - Having a good WR this year has been…pretty much worthless. There is only 1 top-10 WR that is owned by a team over .500, and that is the Madurais’ Tyler Lockett of all people.

Weird stuff with RBs - 2 teams (Shivang and Kishan) each have two top-5 RBs, and RBs 8-12 on the year are Jamaal Williams, Breece Hall, Dameon Pierce, Clyde Edwards-Helaire, and Miles Sanders. I know they always say half of first-round RB picks are busts, but this is wild, and somehow Shivang and Kishan came through the graveyard of expensive auction busts with 2 elite studs each.

Not sure how long each of these trends will last, but needless to say, it has sure made for an interesting 5 weeks to start the year. We’ll see how it all unfolds, but for now, let’s dive into last week’s action.

League Overview

It’s definitely humbling to spend a significant amount of time honing in the power ranking algorithm only for it to remind me week in and week out how awful my team is. I think on balance it does a good job of reflecting what’s happened in the league so far - we’ve got 3 teams that are firing on all cylinders, 2 teams that have been consistently solid, and a long tail of teams that are desperately searching for some consistency.

Last year, each of the playoff teams had at least 3 wins by week 5. The only team that was in playoff position after week 5 but fell out after was Varun, and the person who took his spot was Jeffrey, who was tied with Varun in wins but was losing the tiebreaker on points. Long story short, last year performance in the first 5 weeks was pretty much predictive of who made the playoffs.

Obviously with the new divisions (and the fact that only 4 teams have hit the 3 win threshold after 5 weeks), we’ll see a different story play out this year. But if there’s one thing to be taken away from last year, it’s that every team at 2-3 and 1-4 needs wins and needs them fast.
