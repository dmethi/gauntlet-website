Big time shakeup at the top of the power rankings as this was by far the lowest scoring week of the year. Lot’s of exciting action to unpack, let’s get to it.


Methis [141.90] (3-2) vs Ishan [95.84] (3-2)
No surprises here as mine and Krish’s team bounced back in a huge way against Ishan, who happened to post his worst week of the season.

Absolutely dominant performance this week from my team, as not one player scored less than 8 points. Thielen + Kyler led the way, combining for 50 points, as my team had essentially put this game to bed after the noon games were done. No matter that I benched Miles Sanders who just had his best game of the season — my other running backs each scored 14 points. Nothing spectacular, but it got the job done. We’ll hope to keep this momentum rolling next week as we’ll ride our star-studded lineup against Jeffrey.

Brutal week for Ishan. Not only did he have his worst week of the year, but his superstar QB Dak Prescott had a season-ending injury. Tragic for Dak - not only was he coming off one of the best 4-game QB starts in history, but he also currently has no long-term contract, so this injury will likely cost him tens of millions of dollars. Ishan was so shaken up after this injury that he immediately traded Kenny Golladay (a bona-fide WR1!!!) for Aaron Rodgers to take over his QB slot. Let’s just say the Madurais made out like bandits in this trade. I’m mostly surprised that Ishan traded one of his best WRs after one of the worst weeks anyone has had in terms of WR production. But hey, who knows what’ll happen moving forward. Ishan will certainly hope that his strong and consistent RB production from Jacobs and Carson (and now Bell?) is sufficient to carry him next week in his matchup against Akshay.

Madurais [124.96] (3-2) vs Anant [76.76] (3-2)
After an 0-2 start, the Madurais have rebounded in a big way rattling an impressive 3 straight wins as the injury to Austin Ekeler might have been the exact spark that this team needed. What’s been the secret to their success? Stronger performances from their QBs and RBs, and that recipe was no different this week as Deshaun Watson, Derrick Henry, and Davonta Freeman combined for half of their points for the week. DK Metcalf is looking to be the steal of the draft, as he not only absolutely fucked the Vikings last week, but he continues to be a nightmare for the Madurai’s opponents as he has scored over 17 points in 4 out of the 5 weeks of the season. Now bolstered by the addition of Golladay, the Madurais have come a LONG way from being ranked 11th in the league after week 1. He’ll look to keep rolling in next week’s matchup against Aman.

What a fall from grace for Anant. The loss of Christian McCaffrey has been devastating, as since his loss, Anant has failed to clear 105 points. This week, the bottom truly fell out for Anant as he scored a season low points, one of only a handful of times a team has failed to score 80 points. With Aaron Jones on bye, his patchwork RB corps (Malcom Brown and Duke Johnson) failed to impress as they combined for 6 points. This marks the lowest score all year that someone has received fro their RBs. It also certainly didn’t help that his stud WRs Amari Cooper and Tyler Lockett combined for only 10 points. Despite this brutal week, I’d wager that this marks the season low for Anant. He’s primed for a bounce-back week next week as he not only gets Aaron Jones back but also adds Alexander Mattison off the waiver wire. He’ll hope this leads to better results as he takes on Hunter next week.

Shivang [128.58] (4-1) vs Jeffrey [103.74] (1-4)
Another week, another strong performance for Shivang as he clears 115 points for the 5th straight week, this time scoring a season high. He has scored between 117 and 128 points every single week this season, an unbelievable run of high-performing consistency, and he has rightfully earned his spot at the top of the standings and the top of the power rankings. The shocking part is that he has done this while scoring bottom-2 in the league at the RB position 3 out of 5 weeks of the season, including this week. What’s been the recipe for his success? Strong QB, TE, and special teams play as Shivang has outscored everyone else substantially at the K and DEF positions. That formula held true once again this week, as Russel Wilson, Travis Kelce, and the Ravens defense combined for 70 points. His scoring potential was much higher than the 128 points he scored - he left 3 15-point scorers on his bench and was given the “Worst Manager” award of the week by Sleeper. He’ll look to continue his strong start to the season next week against Varun.

Brutal loss for Jeffrey this week. He actually had a very strong week overall, but the story of this matchup was defined by the combined scoring from the QB and DEF positions. Jeffrey scored 5 points from Matt Ryan and the Chiefs defense while Shivang scored 51 points from those two positions. Tough scenes all around. In any case, every other one of Jeffrey’s position players besides James Robinson scored over 10 points as Mike Davis led the way once again with 25 points. Jeffrey had the best performance of the season from his receivers as DJ Moore and Devante Parker both had solid weeks. He’ll hope to have better luck this week against yours truly, where he needs to pull off the upset win to avoid dropping into a 1-5 hole.

Sitarams [103.02] (3-2) vs Hunter [102.44] (2-3)
What a thrilling victory for the Sitarams. They looked like a complete lock to win this game going into Tuesday night - they were tied with Hunter, and had Josh Allen + Devin Singletary left going into the game against Hunter’s Jonnu Smith. They somehow ended up just scratching by as they were saved by a last minute Josh Allen benching that prevented him from throwing an interception. He was able to overcome Allen/Singletary’s disappointing weeks with strong weeks from Mixon, Henderson, Tyreek, and Evans who continues to be a touchdown machine. They’ll hope a more convincing win next week against Kishan.

Complete heartbreak for Hunter. He thought he had no chance going into Tuesday night, but Jonnu Smith put in a Herculean effort with 18 points but fell just short. Kamara was unable to carry Hunter’s team on his back this time. Shockingly, Kenyan Drake was not the culprit in this one as he put up 10 points for the first time in 3 weeks. The real culprit? Hunter’s WR production, as Kupp and Chark combined for only 12 points, his lowest receiver output for the year. To add insult to injury, Hunter was given the “Best Manager” award by Sleeper as he set his best possible lineup of the week. Just wasn’t enough this time. This marks the 5th straight week that the person who has received the “Best Manager” award by Sleeper has lost. Without Kamara next week, he’ll need an unreal week next week to beat Anant.

Aman [98.60] (3-2) vs Akshay [98.10] (0-5)
Another nail-biting matchup as Aman barely beat out Akshay in his bid for his first win of the season. Aman’s team actually had a decent week aside from his production from his two stud players Lamar Jackson and George Kittle only combined for 20 points. Kareem, Robby Anderson, and Mark Andrews continued to impress as each put up 15 points. Reinforced by the return of Devante Adams, he’ll look to have a much stronger performance next week against the Madurais.

Absolute stinger of a loss for Akshay, who was in striking distance of victory for the first time all season. He was hoping to ride the trend of winning by starting an inactive player (in this case John Brown) but came up just short. Down 45 points coming into Tuesday night, Ryan Tannehill and Stefon Diggs came up just short with Tannehill putting up 30 points. Once again, Akshay’s tight end Logan Thomas gave him no help, scoring just 0.9 points. On the season, Akshay’s tight ends have combined for 7.6 points through 5 weeks. To put that in perspective, 1 catch for a 15-yard touchdown would net a player 8 points. This is an unbelievable run of futility that I think is unparalleled. This marks Akshay’s 9th straight loss, dating back to last season. He’ll look to avoid losing his 10th straight game next week against Ishan.

Varun [108.62] (3-2) vs Kishan [85.60] (2-3)
Light work this week for Varun as Kishan never really posed a threat in this matchup. Patrick Mahomes and Zeke were sufficient to put this game to rest as they combined for 52 points. With Mostert coming back to his lineup, Varun’s team is finally back to full health (at least his starters are). He’ll bring his now-healthy lineup in his next week’s matchup against league top-dog Shivang.

Tough loss for Kishan as only 3 of his starters scored in double digits (Brees, Cook, and Hopkins). To be fair- the rest of his team is straight patchwork as he was forced to start Curtis Samuel, Chris Thompson, and an injured Diontae Johnson. With Julio Jones coming back next week, he’ll need a stronger performance from his team as he takes on the Sitarams next week.
