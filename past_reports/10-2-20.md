Varun [125.9] (1-2) vs Jeffrey [122.02] (1-2)
What a thrilling matchup. This one saw several records being broken. We saw the first 40-point QB performance of the year as well as Jeffrey’s first score over 115 points in a long, long time.

Varun pulled off one of the biggest Monday-night comebacks I’ve ever seen. He was down 25 points against Jeffrey’s Clyde Edwards-Helaire holding on to a sliver of hope with Patty Mahomes remaining. Mahomes decided to pull up a *vintage* performance with 5 total TDs and over 400 total yards, posting a total of exactly 40 points as Varun was able to barely edge Jeffrey by about 4 points, avoiding going into the 0-3 hole by the slimmest of margins. Unbelievable. Varun once again had a very strong performance from his RBs Ezekiel Elliott and Nick Chubb, as this marks the 4th straight week he’s been top-4 in the league in RB production. He successfully employed the bold strategy of starting two WRs from the same team, hitting on Allan Lazard who scored 23 points. Overall, Mr. Autodraft looks to be well positioned yet again (fuck this) as he’s now up to 3rd in the power rankings. However, his momentum may soon come to a screeching halt - with Allan Lazard’s season ending injury, he now is rostering 5 players that are inactive for next week. However, Varun always seemingly defies the odds without even trying so I would expect him to continue to post strong performances and will likely be able to overcome this injury next week in his bye week against Akshay.

What heartbreak for Jeffrey. Despite finally breaking the 115 point plateau, he came up just short on a week where he was the highest scoring team that didn’t win. He started out the week exceptionally with James Robinson going for 27 points on Thursday night. His special teams (32 points from his kicker and defense!!) were able to overcome weak performances from Joe Mixon, DJ Moore, and Darren Waller. The real culprit for Jeffrey was Matt Ryan who only scored 11 points, choking away not only Jeffrey’s matchup but Atlanta’s matchup against Chicago as they threw away a big lead for the second straight week. Once again, smarter lineup decisions by Jeffrey would’ve made this an easy dub as his perfect possible lineup would’ve scored 163 points. He’ll hope to display some smarter team management next week when he faces off against the Madurais.

Kishan [123.12] (2-1) vs Hunter [118.20] (2-1)
Another exceptionally close matchup as Kishan was able to just barely edge out Hunter despite an unbelievable Sunday night charge by Alvin Kamara.

This week was a breakout performance for Kishan, as this marks the highest score he’s posted in his fantasy football career. Starting an inactive Julio Jones did not slow him down one bit, as every one of his players besides Chris Thompson scored in the double digits with Dalvin Cook and DeAndre Hopkins posting vintage performances. The real difference maker for Kishan was his special teams performance - his 33 points from his kicker and defense outscored Hunter’s special teams by 23 points. Kishan finally received above average play from his quarterback, receiving 22 points from Drew Brees marking the first time this season that his QB has scored over 15 points. Despite scoring the 3rd-least points overall on the year, Kishan is well positioned at 2-1 and looks to continue his promising start next week against Aman’s tanking team.

Unbelievably painful loss for Hunter. He came into Sunday night as a long shot to win, needing Alvin Kamara to outscore Drew Brees by 20 points. Alvin Kamara pulled him ever so close, putting up 38 points in one of the most sensational RB performances of the year. It just wasn’t enough, as Hunter still came up 5 points short marking his first loss of his tenure in House Realest. I’m sure Kamara’s back is hurting from carrying the team this week, as there just wasn’t enough support from the rest of Hunter’s team to edge Kishan out as he only had 4 players score in the double digits. Minshew and Kenyan Drake both disappointed big time as they combined for 17 points. This marks the 3rd week in a row that Hunter has received a bottom-2 performance from his QBs - his combined QB points total over 3 weeks is 34.3, 6 points less than the amount Patrick Mahomes put up this week. He’ll need to turn it around next week when he faces Ishan.

Anant [96.38] (3-0) vs Aman [67.28] (1-2)
So many storylines in this matchup. We’ll get to Aman in a second, but first let’s start with Anant’s good fortune this week.

Even though Anant really doesn’t need any help as he currently sits at the top of the power rankings, he received amazing fortune this week as he played one of only two teams that he would’ve beat, as his team severely fell short of expectations scoring more than 70 points less than last week. Tyler Lockett is the only reason Anant posted a respectable score, as his 32 points accounted for 1/3 of Anant’s point total of the week. Cam finally regressed after his sensational performances in weeks 1 and 2, and Dion Lewis + his special teams combined for 3 points. In any case, he still pulled out the dub, and is still sitting at the top of the league heading into this weeks matchup with Shivang’s surging team.

I want to start a recap of Aman’s performance with a quote:

The analytics weren’t wrong about Aman’s team - his team is loaded, and potentially for the first time in his fantasy football career, he looks ready to compete.

That quote is from me. After week 1. Since then, Aman has put up 122 points TOTAL over the last two weeks. God damn it Aman, I really believed in your team this year, and while I had a suspicion that your week 1 performance might be one of the best of the year, I didn’t think it would all come crashing down so quickly. With only 2 players scoring in double digits, Aman’s team really did not put up any semblance of a fight. Kareem Hunt and Corey Davis, his two new acquisitions were the 2nd and 3rd highest scorers on his team so respect to his team management in that regard. Despite his team completely shitting the bed, he still had a shot to win on Monday night as he had Lamar Jackson and Mark Andrews left down 45 points. Given Lamar and Andrews’ chemistry last year and the fact that they were facing the Chiefs in an anticipated shootout, Aman was not out of it at all. Instead, in one of the shocking performances of the year, his two studs combined for only 15 points. Over the past two weeks, Lamar has only scored 30 points which is the amount he basically averaged every week last week. To make his week even worse, Aman was awarded the “Best Manager” of the week award as he set his perfect possible lineup and still only managed to put up 67. Idk about you but I’d be crying myself to sleep if that happened to me. Despite how brutal the last two weeks have been, I still seriously believe in this team. Aman has a real chance to put the disastrous last two weeks behind him - Davante Adams and George Kittle are coming back, Justin Jefferson is added into his lineup, and Lamar and Andrews are probably going to come out this week hunting for blood as they look to put last weeks disappointment behind them. He’ll hope he can turn around his season next week in his matchup against Kishan.

Madurais [142.42] (1-2) vs Ishan [115.38] (2-1)
In one of the more out of nowhere performances of the year, the Madurais put up 140 points on Ishan despite failing to cross 110 in the previous two weeks. They were led by strong performances across the board - Rogers, Henry, and Ekeler each put up 25 points, and Metcalf and Boyd each put up 17. The week could’ve been even better, except Metcalf made one of the most headass mistakes I’ve ever seen in a football game as he fumbled the ball out of the end zone as he was strutting in for a long touchdown. That’s an 8 point swing. In any case, the Madurais are absolutely buzzing as they head into next weeks matchup against Jeffrey. However, they’ll have to take him on without Derrick Henry since the Steelers-Titans matchup was rescheduled because of COVID.

Ishan actually had a bit more disappointing of a week than his score suggests. He was buffered by a sensational 39 points from his special teams (26 from his defense!!). He also had the Patriots on his bench who put up 15 points. I think this is the first time I’ve seen someone roster two defenses that score over 10 points. Ishan clearly is prioritizing special teams as a core component of his winning strategy, as he now is scoring 10 points more from his kicker and defense per week than the league average. Anyways, outside of that performance, there are few brights pots. His RBs combined for the lowest point total in the league, and Jared Cook + Russel Gage combined for only 6 points. However, Dak did continue to steamroll over defenses and Kenny Golladay had a strong season debut as he eases himself into the season. He’ll hope for a more consistent team wide performance in next week’s matchup against Hunter.

Shivang [124.40] (2-1) vs Sitarams [105.84] (1-2)
This was an all-around solid performance by Shivang as none of his players scored under 6 points. While most of his team had ho-hum performances (Godwin, Juju, Kelce, and McKinnon all scored around 15 points), Shivang was carried by his team’s MVP and the presumptive league MVP Russel Wilson, as he scored over 30 points for the third week in a row. He’s receiving 10 more points from his QB position than the league average, and he looks to have drafted a league winner as Russel already has 14 touchdowns on the year and does not look to be slowing down one bit. He’ll be without Godwin next week, but he’ll still hope to keep up this momentum in next week’s showdown against the league top-dog Anant.

The arrow continues to point up for our team as Allen Robinson just received a major upgrade with Nick Foles taking over at quarterback. With a top-5 QB, 3 top-15 RBs, and 3 top-15 WRs, our team can take on any team any week, and we’ll look to keep up this momentum next week against the Sitarams.

Some things just never change, and one of those things seem to be Akshay posting a bottom-3 weekly performance. Honestly, he could’ve had a great week, except Tannehill kept choking in the red zone, Goedert and John Brown got injured, and Ingram just didn’t get any touches as those 4 players combined for just 15 points. Outside of that, Akshay had a pretty solid week with Gurley, Diggs, and Keenan Allen continuing to have strong performances. If you’re looking for a reason to explain Akshay’s disastrous start to the year, look no further than his QB and TE performances - he’s second to last in QB points scored, and dead last in TE points scored. He’s received 3 points total from his TEs ALL YEAR. That’s unbelievable, you just can’t make that stuff up. With a strong and deep WR corps, I would suggest Akshay look towards offloading some of his receivers for help at QB/TE. If he’s able to upgrade at those positions and with Michael Thomas coming back Week 5, I think Akshay could make a midseason run to put himself into contention.

In terms of consistency, Ishan, Shivang, and Hunter are at the top of the list. They’ve scored over 110 points in every single week this year.

Expected win % measures your win % based on if you played every single team every single week of the year. I was shocked to see that at the top of the list isn’t Anant, but it’s Varun…but he currently has a record of 1-2. His opponents are averaging 146 points against him per week :O. That’s unbelievably bad luck, and I fully expect to see him rebound soon. On the other hand, Akshay has by far the lowest expected win % at only 9%. The next lowest is 33%.

The spread at QB is insane this year - if you look at Shivang vs Hunter (top scoring vs bottom scoring at QB), Shivang has outscored Hunter by almost 70 points in 3 weeks at that position - that’s more than 23 points per week, which is a little over the league average for scoring at QB. Shivang has such an advantage over him, it’s as if he’s starting an extra QB.

The RB position is the epitome of the haves and the have nots - 4 players have received less than 55 points total from their top-2 RBs in the first 3 weeks, while 5 have received over 100 points total from their top-2 RBs.

There have been two weeks where Varun’s kicker has scored 1 point or less. No one else in the league has one such week. Lol.

TE scoring was absolutely brutal this week, as the average TE scored only 6.3 points and the highest scoring TE scored only 11.7 points. 4 starting TEs scored 3 points or less.
