Power rankings + forecasts
Tier 1 - (Near) Playoff locks
Shivang (5-1) - No change. Playoff probability: 96.2%

Top scorer in the league, he’s absolutely rolling right now. He’s yet to face any real adversity as he’s had the league’s easiest schedule and he hasn’t beat someone that has scored 105 points yet. In any case, the hallmark of his current season is consistency - he’s the only player that has scored above league average every single week this season. He’s benefitted from #letrusscook and the best TE in the game, but more than anything, he’s been carried by ELITE special teams as he’s received 30 more points from his K and DEF than anyone else in the league. There are some holes in his team - he’s dead last in RB scoring and middle of the pack in WR scoring, and hopefully the acquisition of Clyde will help with that. He doesn’t have a cakewalk remaining for the rest of the season - he’s got 4 matchups still left against teams in the top half of the power rankings - so he’ll certainly have to earn his way into the playoffs.

Madurais (4-2) - Last Week: 4. Playoff probability: 85.32%

What a dramatic turnaround after a rough 1st couple of weeks for the Madurais - they’ve been steadily moving up the power rankings every single week with an eye on the #1 spot, and are currently tied with Shivang for the longest win streak in the league. Driven by strong QB (over 25 points each of the last 4 weeks), elite RB scoring the past 4 weeks (10 more points per week verses league average), and strong WR production (3rd best in WR scoring the last 4 weeks), they are absolutely FIRING on all cylinders despite the loss of Austin Ekeler. With only 3 matchups left against teams that are over .500 on the year, the Madurais look to be a near-lock to make the playoffs this year after missing it in the last week last year.

Methis (4-2) - No change. Playoff probability: 84.44%

As one of only 3 teams this season with 3 scores over 120 on the year, our team has an enormous ceiling and currently have the 2nd most points scored on the year. We also have a top-2 weekly score in 3 of the last 4 weeks. Yep, we’re on fire. The QB and WR positions have been a consistent source of strength, but we’re still struggling with finding a consistent RB combination (only 1 week with over 30 points from my RBs) and a TE that can score (still have yet to start a TE that has scored over 10 points, only person in the league). The next 3 weeks are an absolute gauntlet for us as we’re facing 3 current playoff teams, but I’m confident we’ll emerge victorious in each matchup.

Tier 2 - Jockeying for seeding
Ishan (4-2) - Last week: 5. Playoff probability: 79.96%

It’s been a tale of two halves for the newcomer. He started with 4 straight weeks of 115+ scores but that’s been followed by 2 straight weeks failing to hit 100 points. It was certainly an impressive early stretch for Ishan as despite his brutal early-season schedule (2nd-hardest in the league), he came out with 2 wins against teams that scored 120+ points - the rest of the league has 2 such wins combined. Since the Dak injury, his QB scoring has absolutely crashed as he’s only scored 16 points from the last two weeks combined from the position. His RB position remains an enormous strength as he’s had 5 weeks where is RBs have scored over 30 points together - that’s the most in the league. He is, however, bottom half of the league in WR scoring, definitely not made easier by the fact that he shipped of Golladay. TE scoring is also a disaster, but with 3 straight matchups against non-playoff teams, he has a real chance to right the ship quickly.

Sitarams (4-2) - Last week: 7. Playoff probability: 69.2%

This is a very inconsistent team that’s lucky to be at 4-2 as they have 2 wins after scoring below league average for the week. Their expected win percentage (i.e. total win % if they had played every team in the league every week) is only 47%, which means they’ve been gifted an extra win by the schedule they’ve played. They’ve only scored over league average 2 times this season. Regardless, they’re still 4-2 and have a good shot at making the playoffs. They’ve been carried by strong QB scoring which has tailed off as of late, and RB scoring has been unaffected by the Saquon injury, impressive job rebounding from that seemingly insurmountable setback. Overall, they’ve been exceptionally mediocre at all positions, sitting between 4th and 8th in the league in total scoring at every single position. They will need a breakout somewhere on their roster for them to have a real shot at the ship.

Aman (3-3) - Last week: 10. Playoff probability: 34.68%

Extremely up and down year for Aman, 2 weeks over 140, two weeks under 70, and 2 weeks in between. He hasn’t been able to really take advantage of the 3rd-easiest schedule in the league. QB is his biggest problem area - Lamar Jackson has not been as advertised, and he sits below average in league scoring - will need a huge turnaround from his stud. Despite opting for a zero-RB strategy (literally), Aman has turned it around with 3 straight weeks of over 30 points from that position, good for 3rd in the league in that timespan. After a thunderous showing in week 1 from his receivers, Aman has failed to hit 20 points from that group in each of the last 5 weeks - he’s scored 40 points less than the next worst player at that position over the last 5 weeks. That should change with Devante Adams coming back from injury. His TE draft strat of drafting 2 studs looks to be paying off, and the only thing that might slow him down is his defense where over the last 5 weeks, he’s scored a cumulative 5 points from that position (including 2 weeks of negative performances) which is just unreal. The rest of the league has 2 negative defensive weeks combined. He’s got a brutal upcoming schedule as he has 4 straight matchups against current playoff teams, but will need to make a run to not put himself out of position.

Hunter (2-4) - Last week: 6. Playoff probability: 19.24%

Unbelievably brutal luck for Hunter as he’s lost two times after scoring over 115 points - rest of the league has 4 such losses combined. He’s one of only 2 players that hasn’t scored in the bottom-5 in league scoring in any week, yet also hasn’t shown top-end upside with only 1 top-3 weekly finish (which he tragically lost) - he has the highest expected wins in losses in the season. His losses have also been heartbreaking - only person with an average margin of victory in the single digits with 3 losses within 5 points. QB scoring has been the biggest issue - Hunter has lost 7 points every week to league average (which would have made the difference in 3 of his losses!!!). In another world with average QB play, he’s sitting at the top of the league at 5-1. RB scoring is tops in the league and TE scoring isn’t an issue, but WR scoring is another area of concern as he sits as 2nd-to last in this category as well. With only 3 matchups against current playoff teams left in the season, he’s not out of it yet and has a great shot of making a run.

Anant (4-2) - No change. Playoff probability: 84.04%

It’s remarkable that he’s still at 4-2 despite losing McCaffrey after week 2. He has certainly benefited from a soft schedule the past 4 weeks - despite scoring only 5 points more than Akshay during that stretch, he’s 2-2. Shaky QB + TE play as of late has the only chance of holding him back as he currently has the 2nd-best WR corps in the league, and when McCaffrey comes back, he will have likely the most lethal RB duo in the league as well. After this week, only has 1 more matchup left against a team that is currently in the top half of the league - the stage is set for another playoff appearance for Mr. Jindani.

Varun (3-3) - Last week: 2. Playoff probability: 33.8%

Mr. Autodraft has had a solid start to the season despite having the most points scored against him. With 3 weeks over 120 points, he certainly has one of the more explosive lineups, but if this week was any indication, it also has a very low floor. QB, RB, and TE are relative strengths, but league worst production at WR has him shopping Zeke for answers at the position. With 5 matchups left against current playoff teams, Varun certainly has his work cut out for him to earn himself a spot in the playoffs.

Keenan Allen (WR) for Robert Tonyan (TE) + Tee Higgins (WR)

Methis: A, Akshay: B-

I’m not going to lie, this was a kind of one sided trade. To be fair, these are the kinds of risks Akshay needs to take if he wants to put himself back in contention. The reason that this is a little lopsided is because Keenan Allen was traded at his likely lowest value of the season, while Tonyan and Higgins were bought at their likely highest value. But regardless of value, if Higgins is able to come close to Allen’s production and Tonyan plays like the stud he’s been playing like on an efficient Packer’s offense, this is a win for Akshay as he sorely needed an upgrade at TE.

Aaron Rodgers (QB) for Kenny Golladay (WR)

Madurais: A+, Ishan: D

Bruh, what????? A top-10 WR for a QB not named Mahomes or Russel Wilson???? Struggling to understand the rationale of this trade. With QBs such as Minshew, Garoppolo, and Dalton still on the waiver wire, trading a bona-fide stud for a small upgrade over those folks makes little sense to me, and I can’t envision a scenario where this trade pays off for Ishan unless Rodgers literally scores 30 points a game for the rest of the season (he’s downgrading from Golladay to DeAndre Swift in his starting lineup). The Madurais made out like bandits in this one, getting rid of their backup QB for a stud WR to pair with DK Metcalf.

Robert Woods (WR) + Clyde Edwards-Helaire (RB) for Chris Godwin (WR) + DeVante Parker (WR) + Hunter Renfrow (WR)

Shivang: A, Jeffrey: C+

Great trade for Shivang as he was finally able to offload a couple of the 30 WRs he had on his roster for some meaningful help at the RB position. Obviously the Bell trade changes things, but in any case it’s a substantial upgrade over McKinnon/White, and receiving Woods to help soften the blow of losing Godwin certainly helps. For this trade to pay off for Jeffrey, he’ll need Godwin to perform as a top-3 fantasy WR to make up for the huge loss in RB depth (he didn’t receive any RBs back!!). Right now, it seems like he traded away Clyde for a marginal upgrade from Woods to Godwin and a marginal upgrade from DJ Moore to Parker. That’s a HUGE price, and this trade has already cost Jeffrey a win this week.

Joe Mixon (RB) for Melvin Gordon (RB) + Julian Edelman (WR)

Jeffrey: F, Aditya: A+

Aditya receives a top-10 RB in exchange for an RB coming off what will likely be the best week of his season (against the Jets) and a WR that neither Aditya nor Jeffrey will be starting 99% of the time. What a fucking steal. On the other hand, this trade makes NEGATIVE sense for Jeffrey, especially considering that it came after his trade with Shivang from who he had already received 3 WRs. He downgraded at RB for a benchwarmer??? Like what??? Absolutely baffling, and there is absolutely zero chance this trade ends up being a win for Jeffrey long-term.

Adam Thielen (WR) for Kareem Hunt (RB) + Corey Davis (WR)

Aman: A+, Methis: A

First trade of the season was an enormous win-win for both teams. Aman was in desperate need of a RB, and I received a player that was instantly slotted into my lineup as an elite WR. At the time of the trade, I thought it was a lot more favorable for me, but after the Chubb injury (and for the whole season tbh), Hunt has been a top-6 RB in the league. He’s been a stud at a position which Aman sorely needed help at. Meanwhile, Thielen has been a mainstay in my starting lineup and I am more than happy to have him.
