7 weeks in, and this is already certified one of the strangest football seasons in recent memory. There are barely any true ‘breakouts’ this year (with the exception of like Ken Walker and Breece Hall), teams are getting rewarded for drafting boring players (Josh Jacobs, Nick Chubb, etc.), and it’s the QB position of all positions that’s giving teams a massive edge during a year in which you’re lucky if your QB hits 20 points (which is so far from the norm).

Despite the craziness of the first few weeks, the standings are still quite congested. Besides Shivang, every single team has between 2 and 5 wins, and with only 5 teams above .500 on the year, no team is more than 1 game out of a playoff spot. That sets the stage for a competitive and chaotic second half of the year where pretty much nothing is guaranteed. It’s gonna be fun to watch everything play out.

In the meantime, let’s recap last week’s action.
