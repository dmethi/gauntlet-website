Madurais [104.10] (5-2) vs Varun [83.80] (3-4)
Despite essentially getting two donuts from Freeman and Higbee, the Madurais still came out with a comfortable victory over Varun led by Deshaun Watson and Tyler Boyd (who combined for almost half of their points). Now winners of 5 in a row, the Madurais have positioned themselves in the drivers seat for a deep playoff run. They’ll have a challenge keeping the win streak up as they’re facing yours truly next week. Varun, on the other hand, has seen his season turn completely sideways the last two weeks. With a season-ending injury to OBJ, the all-IR team took another blow as he has lost Mostert, Lazard, Chubb, and now Odell for extended periods of time. Mahomes and Zeke no longer look like the studs of last year, as this week they only combined for 17 points. After an earth shattering 2019 season for Mahomes, this year has been a completely different story as he has only cleared 25 points 3 times this year. With the loss of Dak, Zeke and the Cowboys offense look absolutely horrendous. Despite all this, Varun still sits with a reasonable shot at the playoffs, but he needs to turn things around fast. He’ll look to start that with a win against Ishan next week.

Shivang [89.82] (5-2) vs Aman [122.12] (4-3)
Great bounce-back week for Aman as he easily took down the league top-dog Shivang who never really put up a fight in this one. Every one of his players had a solid week, and Davante Adams’ 38 points were more than enough to take him over the top as he rattled off the 3rd-highest score of the week. He’ll look for Davante Adams to continue to feast against the non-existent secondary Vikings defense in his next week’s matchup against the Sitarams. The floor completely fell out from under Shivang as only 3 of his players scored in double digits. The loss of the Ravens special teams was indeed devastating for him as he scored 10 points less from his special teams than he does on average. Now sitting at 5-2 on the season, he’s still very well situated to make the playoffs and will hope to bounce back next week against Hunter.

Sitarams [99.18] (4-3) vs Akshay [126.10] (1-6)
THE LAD HAS DONE IT. For the first time in over a year, Akshay has finally one a game in DOMINANT fashion. With only one starter on his team in single digits, Akshay had an all around sensational performance, and newcomers Amari Cooper and Tee Higgins combined for 30 points. His team easily put away the Sitarams and put the league on notice - Akshay is no longer a bye week folks. He’ll look to build on this breakout performance next week against Anant. Brutal week for the Sitarams as their special teams accounted for 30% of their points and only 2 of their starters scored in double digits. Josh Allen has cooled off significantly, failing to clear 17 points in each of the last 3 weeks. The Sitarams will need a better performance next week against Aman.

Kishan [85.02] (2-5) vs Ishan [101.12] (5-2)
Very meh performance by Ishan who was carried by Rodgers and AJ Brown who combined for half of his points. It ended up being enough in this one as he was able to beat Kishan by a comfortable margin. Ishan was so excited about his QB that he started talking smack about his trade grade in the chat - but he forgot about the fact that not only was Rodgers only the QB8 for Week 7, but he wasn’t even the highest scoring QB on his team (Wentz was on his bench). He’s had an impressive season thus far, and his only 2 losses have come when he’s faced the highest scoring team of the week. He’ll look to keep the momentum rolling next week against Varun. Tough scene for Kishan - if he would’ve started Diontae Johnson over Jerry Jeudy, he would’ve won. The loser of 4 in a row, Kishan needs to turn his season around quickly for a shot at the playoffs. He’ll have Cook back for his next week’s matchup against Jeffrey.

Hunter [99.84] (2-5) vs Jeffrey [130.22] (2-5)
Sensational week for Jeffrey - even though Claypool scored -0.7 points, his team put up 130 points as James Robinson, Godwin, and the Football Team Defense combined for 70 points. In typical Jeffrey fashion, he once again traded away his stud RB as he shipped off James Robinson for Michael Thomas. He’s a FIEND for the WRs. He’ll hope to continue making a late season charge in his battle against Kishan this week. Another disappointing performance for Hunter as he was unable to overcome the DJ Chark, Jonnu Smith, and Kenyan Drake’s dud performances as he lost his 5th matchup in a row. His RBs continue to be a source of strength, but he’s getting absolutely railed by his WR scoring as he now has the 2nd lowest WR points on the season from that position. He’ll need to turn it around fast to have a shot at playoff relevance, and he’ll hope to break his losing streak next week against Shivang.

Trade grades
Ezekiel Elliot (RB) for Chase Edmonds (RB)/Tyler Boyd (WR)

Varun: A, Madurais: A-

Win-win trade for both players. The Madurais had the depth to take a chance on a 2nd-half Zeke rebound while Varun was able to acquire a couple of players who are absolutely rolling and will help him win now.

Michael Thomas (WR)/ Philip Lindsay (RB) for James Robinson (RB)

Jeffrey: B, Akshay: A

Sensational trade for Akshay - he was able to offload an injured and discontented receiver (he was already stacked at receiver anyways) to acquire a bona-fide RB1 who is currently the second highest scoring RB in the league. A little more questionable for Jeffrey - he traded his best RB (his next best RB is Melvin Gordon) and took a chance on Michael Thomas. This might make sense for a team that’s 5-2 and can afford to take some risks, but not for a 2-5 team that needs to win now. We’ll have to see how this one works out.

Darren Waller (TE)/DJ Moore (WR) for Tyler Higbee (TE)/ DK Metcalf (WR)

Jeffrey: A+, Madurais: B-

Great trade for Jeffrey as he clearly got the best player involved in the trade in DK Metcalf and was able to sell high on DJ Moore who has seen his target share in Carolina diminish. A little more questionable for the Madurais who paid a heavy premium for an upgrade at the TE position. So far this season, only 1 TE has scored as many points as a top-20 WR which brings into question the strategy of trading for a TE. Might as well take a dart throw on random TEs from week to week and hope they score a touchdown while keeping a dominant receiver as a starter.
