Fantasy football is so strange. One week after self pronouncing myself dead in the water, my team showed some inspiring resilience putting up nearly 140 points without CMC, led by (as we all expected) Cordarelle Patterson who put up 33 points on 11 touches. Wtf. Mine and Krish’s team has been quite hit or miss this season, twice scoring under 110 points and twice scoring over 130. Hopefully future weeks will bring in some more consistency from our team. Now back to .500 on the year, we’ll be looking to keep the good mojo going next week against Shivang.

After 3 weeks of steadily increasing scores and climbing the leaderboard, it all came crashing down for Aman this week as he posted his lowest score of the year. Yes the matchup gods haven’t been kind to him (this is the 4th straight week his opponent scored above league average), but he’d have lost this week pretty much no matter who he played. There’s lots of blame to be passed around for this performance, but the chief gameday ducks for Aman were Tom Brady (who looked p rattled in his return to Foxborough this week) and Dalvin Cook (don’t get me started on the Vikings). Like our team, Aman’s team has also been following a boom-bust pattern, with two weeks above 120 and 2 weeks below 90. He’ll be looking to steady the ship next week against Kishan. 

Sitarams [124.28] (3-1) vs Madurais [108.42] (3-1)
Line: U 239.4, Sitarams covered -5.4

What was gearing up to be a heater of a matchup turned into quite an easy victory for the Sitarams as Herbert and Waller’s solid performances on Monday night led them to a comfortable win. James Robinson and Scary Terry’s monstrous performances were more than enough to overcome disappointing performances by Thielen and Kupp to push the Sitarams to 3-1 on the year. They’ve been an unbelievable model of consistency this year, scoring between 120 and 125 points in every single matchup having either 8 or 9 expected wins (out of a maximum of 11) riding pretty much the same exact starting lineup week in week out. They’ll look to keep it rolling next week in their matchup against Ishan.

Disappointing performance for the Madurais. After Zeke and Henry laid out a solid foundation during the noon games, all of their receivers disappointed in a big way scoring well below their projections. A fluke 20 point performance from Dawson Knox was the only thing standing in the way of a truly humiliating performance. Now no longer undefeated, the Madurais fall to 3-1 on the year and will look to get back into the win column next week in Akshay. 

Varun [118.44] (2-2) vs Anish [100.90] (1-3)
Line: O 218.8, Varun covered +4.6

In a matchup pitting the two future Bay Area roommates against one another, Varun came out on top in this one, led by a sensational special teams showing in which his kicker and defense outscored Anish’s 36.8 to -3. Big yikes on that one. This had the makings of a monster fantasy day for Varun if Baker Mayfield didn’t repeatedly miss wide open throws to OBJ, but he still enjoyed a comfortable victory thanks to solid weeks from Metcalf, Henderson, Fant, and Lamar. 2-2 on the year, Varun has managed to stay afloat despite failing to clear 120 points yet. He’ll hope to change that next week against Anant.

Tough showing for Anish whose special teams cost him dearly in this one. With average special teams, say 7 points from his defense and 7 points from his kicker, this matchup would’ve been an absolute nailbiter, but instead, Anish falls to 1-3 and is still searching for his first score over 105 points. With the emergence of Clyde and DJ Moore, things are trending up, and he’ll hope this positive momentum results in a blowup week next week against Jeffrey as he looks to avoid falling to 1-4 on the year.

Another blowup week that came out of nowhere, Shivang nearly outscored his past two weeks totals’ combined led by Sam Darnold, DMont, and a lethal WR trio that combined for nearly 50 points. Up to this point, Shivang has failed to take advantage of by far the easiest schedule in the league this week — none of his opponents have cleared 100 points, yet Shivang is still only 2-2 on the year. Lot of season left for him to bounce back, and he’ll look to build upon this breakout performance next week against Krish and I, where he will be without his stud RB (rip dmont).

As the only manager that has yet to make a transaction this year, Kishan probably should step out of the press box and head to the sidelines as his team may require a more active management strategy to avoid quickly falling into irrelevance. We’ll see if Kishan has any moves up his sleeve for his next week’s matchup against Aman where he’ll be looking to pick up his first win. 

Ishan [93.50] (1-3) vs Akshay [111.52] (4-0)
Line: U 227.5, Akshay covered +3.9

Another week, another letdown for Ishan as his team failed to cross 90 points for the second time this year. This is the second straight week that injuries have affected his starters as Teddy Bridgewater and Logan Thomas had to exit early in their games, and the result likely would’ve been quite a bit closer if they were healthy (Ishan would’ve won if both players hit their projected total). The biggest issue for Ishan’s team this far into the season has been the lack of blow up performances - thus far, only two of his starters has scored over 20 points in a week this year, one being Teddy in Week 2 with a 21 point performance and one being Ekeler this week. Despite having one of the easier schedules in the league, Ishan falls to 1-3 on the year and will likely need a huge performance next week against the Sitarams if he wants to pick up his second win of the year. 

Surprisingly, Akshay has been a model of consistency as well, recording his 4th score between 110 and 125 points. Patty Mahomes and Najee Harris led the way for Akshay in this one as he continues to find ways to win, moving to 4-0 on the year and sitting at the top of the league as the only undefeated team left. Usually in fantasy, winning 4 straight matchups requires an element of luck and that certainly has been the case so far for Akshay as even though he’s scored above league average every single week, the chances of him being 4-0 on the year based on other results around the league are only 17%. Either way, his team is playing well and he’ll look to stay undefeated next week against the Madurais. 

Anant [79.26] (1-3) vs Jeffrey [146.62] (3-1)
Line: U 227.5, Jeffrey covered +2.1

Wow, what an absolute beatdown of Anant by Jeffrey. While Anant’s struggles at RB have been well documented (20 carries for 27 yards between Sanders and Davis 🤮), his stud WRs and TE let him down in this one as Chase, CeeDee, and Hockenson combined for only 22 points. While they’ll probably be fine, I’m sure changes are on the horizon at RB for Anant as he had 3 bench RBs score over 15 points. Anant’s team is trending in the completely wrong direction, decreasing in scoring for the 3rd week in a row, and he’ll hope to find the right combination in his starting lineup to get out of this slump in his next week’s matchup against Varun to avoid falling to 1-4 on the year. 

What a week for Jeffrey as Saquads and Deebo went absolutely nuclear in this one, combining for nearly 60 points powering Jeffrey to be the highest scorer of the week. The only question mark for Jeffrey’s team coming into the season was injuries, and unfortunately for him, they’re starting to add up. Gronk, Julio, and Tee Higgins have already hit the shelf, and now you can add Joe Mixon to that crew as he’s week-to-week with a bum ankle (where have I heard that before…?). Only time will tell if this was a ‘Last Dance’ performance for his team or whether this performance is a sign of things to come, we’ll find out next week in his matchup against Anish.
