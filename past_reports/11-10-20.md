
After a disastrous opening stretch to start the season, the Vikings have suddenly won 2 games in a row against division rivals now facing a stretch of winnable games. Within the span of just a couple of weeks the Vikings have made the season interesting again at the same time that our fantasy football season is really starting to heat up. The next few weeks of football are going to be so much fun.

Methis [105.82] (4-5) vs Shivang [116.50] (7-2)
Another week, another standout performance for Shivang as this marks the 8th time in 9 weeks that he has cleared 110 points, a remarkable stretch of consistency. The fact that he’s been able to do this while being dead last in the league in RB scoring is wild. He’s able to make up for that by having the highest scoring TE in the league by a MILE - Kelce is giving him an extra 10 points per week over league average at the position, which is unreal. This week was no different as Kelce put up 10 catches for 160 yards. Now officially a lock for teh playoffs, Shivang will look to continue rolling next week against Akshay.

Madurais [71.84] (6-3) vs Hunter [97.00] (3-6)
The lad has done it! After 6 straight weeks of taking Ls and having brutal matchup luck (only person to face 6 straight opponents who scored over 100 points), Hunter finally catches a break and faces the lowest scoring team in the league and finally puts himself in the win column for the first time since Week 2.

Happy Birthday Reaction GIF
It doesn’t matter that DJ Chark is his only player that scored over 15 points or that Kamara only scored 13 in a week the Saints offense scored 5 touchdowns - all that matters is he’s clinging on to the sliver of hope he has for the playoffs. Now that the monkey is finally off his back, he’ll look to keep this momentum rolling next week in his matchup against Varun.

What a fall from grace for the Madurais. They’ve now firmly lost their midseason form as this is the second week in a row that they’ve failed to score 75 points. Given their putrid showing the last couple of weeks, you’d almost forget that they averaged 125 points over a 4-week stretch from Weeks 3-6. What’s been the biggest difference? Their WR scoring. Over the first 7 weeks of the season, they averaged 30 points per week from their top 2 wideouts. In the last two weeks, they’ve averaged 5. That’s what happens when you trade away DK Metcalf.

Sitarams [113.70] (4-5) vs Varun [114.78] (5-4)
Another thrilling matchup, and already the 8th time this season that a matchup has been decided by less than 5 points. In this case, it was only 1.08, as Varun BARELY edged out Aditya to 5-4 on the year. Christian Kirk was the surprise standout that helped push Varun to a victory - he needed every bit of Kirk’s 5 catch/123 yard/1 TD effort. This was a crucial victory for him as he will likely be reinforced by the return of Nick Chubb next week when he’ll face Hunter.

Another week, another heartbreak for the Sitarams. This one is an especially tough loss to swallow given that Josh Allen and Tyreek Hill went absolutely nuclear, combining for 64 points. There’s plenty of blame to go around:

JaMychal Hasty only touching the ball 6 times, turning it into 13 yards

Devin Singletary only touching the ball 5 times (despite the Bills scoring 44 points!!!)

Tom Brady missing a “wide open” Mike Evans many times (the Buccaneers coach literally called Brady out on it) and possibly having the worst game of his career

The Patriots Defense, scoring only 3 points against a now 0-9 Jets team that had previously allowed at least 6 points to every defense they had played against

Long story short, victory was well within reach, but instead, they slip to 4-5 (3rd straight loss) and are now currently sitting outside of a playoff spot. Like Krish and I, they need a win BADLY next week in their matchup against Jeffrey.

Kishan [112.48] (3-6) vs Anant [135.86] (7-2)
Highest scoring matchup of the week did not disappoint as Anant came out on top in this one. Christian McCaffrey did not disappoint in his return to the lineup and provided Anant an enormous boost, scoring 32 points in his first game back. With strong performances from Diggs, Fuller, and Cam Newton, Anant was the top scorer in the league for the 2nd time in the last 3 weeks and has essentially locked up a playoff spot. Winner of 4 straight, he’ll look to continue his dominant stretch next week against Ishan.

Tough loss for Kishan as he had a great week for the second week in a row. Dalvin continued to be the heart and soul of Kishan’s team, and Dalvin’s last 2-week stretch has been the most dominant 2-game stretch of any fantasy player in a very long time. It wasn’t enough for him this week as disappointing weeks from both Julio and Hopkins sunk him in this one. Sitting at 3-6, Kishan is down but not out and has shown lots of promise the last two weeks. He’ll need to convert this recent momentum into wins, and that needs to start next week against the sinking Madurais.

Ishan [110.20] (6-3) vs Aman [82.60] (5-4)
Light work for Ishan as Aman’s team never really put up a fight in this one. He was able to overcome Corey Davis’ donut with strong weeks from Aaron Rodgers (still don’t think it was a great trade), AJ Brown, and Terry McLaurin. After scoring over 115 for each of the first four weeks, Ishan has yet to score over 110 since then, but he’s still managed to go 3-2 during that stretch. He’ll need a more dominant showing next week when he takes on Anant.

Tough, tough week for Aman. The up and down season continues as this is the 5th time that Aman’s team has failed to reach 100 points - only Kishan and Akshay have had as many sub-100 point performances as him. Nothing went right this week - the Lamar/Andrews connection was nonexistent, Ronald Jones did jack shit, and Justin Jackson was ruled out for the game after 2 plays. Davante Adams was the lone bright spot for Aman this week as he is far and away the most dominant receiver in fantasy football. He’ll hope for a more complete team performance next week as he takes on Krish and I.

Jeffrey [91.34] (3-6) vs Akshay [86.62] (1-8)
Jeffrey is so lucky that he won this one - a loss might’ve ended his career. Coming into Sunday night, he needed Michael Thomas and Chris Godwin to outscore Gronk by 5 points, and they BARELY did that — he was saved by Gronk dropping a TD. DK Metcalf, Justin Herbert, and his special teams combined for almost 80% of his points. That’s right, his remaining 5 players (including 2 RBs, 2 WRs, and a TE) combined for 20 points. Either way, he came out with the dub and hangs on to a very slim hope to qualify for the playoffs. He’ll need a win next week against the Sitarams to continue his playoff push as a loss next week likely knocks him out.

If last week’s loss didn’t eliminate Akshay from playoff contention, this one certainly did. This is the 8th time that Akshay has scored in the bottom-3 in weekly scoring. Hey, at least he’s consistent. At this point, Akshay is just playing for pride and moral victories as he looks to avoid the unprecedented fate of finishing dead last in the league 2 years in a row. In one of the most lopsided matchups of the year, he’ll be taking on Shivang next week. A victory this week would definitely be a huge morale boost for the franchise - this is essentially Akshay’s Super Bowl as it’s his most high-profile matchup of the year. I’d call it a dub for Akshay if he avoids getting beat by 50.
