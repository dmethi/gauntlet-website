Methis (96.28) [6-3] vs Anant (87.80) [4-5]
Despite Pat Friermuth’s 19 point performance on Monday night, this was a relatively comfortable win for us as we put up a top-4 performance for the 5th time in 6 weeks. This was truly a team effort as we were able to get to this point total without a player scoring over 17 points. Cordarelle and Bobby Trees were as consistent as ever in this one, and CMC provided a much needed 12 point boost in his limited return to action, helping overcome relatively disappointing weeks from Mark Andrews and Amari Cooper. Now riding a 3-game win streak, we’re almost in a win-and-in situation, 1 game away from securing a season-ending record of at least .500. Our sights are set much higher than that.

Pretty resilient performance for Anant who had to deal with several playmakers being out on either bye or with injury. This certainly was a winnable game, but his core playmakers Ja’Marr Chase and CeeDee Lamb let him down big time, combining for only 9 points. A win would’ve been an absolute steal, though, as 3 of Anant’s top 4 scorers were his kicker, defense, and Pat Friermuth. Currently sitting on the outside of playoff contention looking in, Anant’s got a bit of an uphill battle ahead of him to lock up a playoff spot.

Madurais (86.38) [7-2] vs Jeffrey (89.76) [6-3]
The first week of post-Henry life did not treat the Madurais well as they put up their lowest score of the year. The loss of Henry specifically was notable as they scored their least amount of points from the RB position since Week 1. Like with Anant, their studs let them down big time in this one with Adams and Zeke combining for only 18 points. Despite their 4 game win streak coming to an end, the Madurais are still essentially locked in for a playoff spot, and they’ll just be looking for the right combination of players in their starting lineup to build some momentum for a championship run.

Despite underperformances across his roster, Mixon and Kittle did just enough for Jeffrey to barely squeeze by in this matchup securing him his 6th win of the year. Those two combined for nearly 50% of Jeffrey’s points this week. It’s quite impressive that Jeffrey has won 3 of his last 4 games given the numerous injuries he’s had to deal with, but with Kittle’s return and Saquon’s return on the horizon, things seem to be looking up for the health of his team, although with those players, you never really know. Like with Krish and I, Jeffrey is 1 win away from virtually locking up a playoff spot, sitting in a prime win and in situation.

Varun (106.14) [5-4] vs Ishan (89.36) [2-7]
Big win for Varun, who scored the 2nd most points in the league en route to moving back to 1 game over .500. Lamar and Chubb were the heroes this week for Varun, combining for half his points - they were the 4th and 6th highest scoring players of the week. He’s been alternating wins and losses since Week 5, but his #FREEOBJ prayers have finally been answered with his move to the Rams. He’ll be hoping that change of scenery injects some momentum into his team since even though he’s the current 5th seed, he’s still a couple wins away from securing a playoff spot.

Tough loss for Ishan who would’ve beat 6 other teams this week. The last 3 weeks have been particularly rough in terms of a matchup standpoint - he’s faced the 3rd, 2nd, and 2nd highest scoring teams the last 3 weeks. Right when he really needed to make a run too, that’s gotta hurt. In any case, Ishan’s team is on a big downwards spiral, scoring decreasing amounts of points every week since Week 5. Considering the fact that his Defense was the only player who scored over 14 points for him, there aren’t many signs from his team that this troubling trend will stop.

Kishan (43.26) [1-8] vs Anish (80.88) [3-6]
The first week that Kishan actually sets his roster and changes out his kicker and defense, his team puts up a disgusting 43 points driven in part by Kishan starting an inactive DeAndre Hopkins and the fact that his new and fresh kicker and defense combined for -1 points. It’s as if the world is telling Kishan his team is better left without his intervention. Ouch. In the process of setting the record for the lowest number of points scored this week, Kishan also officially shut the door on his already slim playoff chances. A fitting end to a campaign marred by managerial ineptitude and lost potential.

Somehow, some way, the magician does it again, securing his 3rd victory of the season despite never scoring over 105 points. He’s averaging only 90 points per game in his wins - if you extend that out to a full season up to this point, that would be good for only 810 points which would be dead last in the league (which means that somehow, Anish is averaging more points in his losses than his wins…). This is now the 5th time this year that Anish has either been the lowest or 2nd lowest scoring team of the week. Despite all that, he’s only 2 games out of 5th place with 4 games left in the season, so the door is still wide open for him to make a charge, and the injury to Chase Edmunds might just be the opening that Anish has been looking for to finally break out.

Sitarams 👑 (121.94) [7-2] vs Shivang (96.88) [4-5]
Quickly bouncing back from a relatively disappointing performance last week, the Sitarams quickly put themselves back in their comfort zone, scoring 120+ points for the 7th time this year. They’ve now been the top scorer 3 times out of the last 4 weeks and been a top-4 scorer 7 times this season. Their season has been so impressive that they’ve outscored all but 2 teams’ OPTIMAL lineups on the year. Basically, if every other team set the perfect lineup every week and left no points on their bench, the Sitarams would still be 3rd overall in points scored. Their consistency has been unbelievable, and this week’s performance was particularly impressive considering their special teams combined for -2 points. It certainly helps to have the #2 and #3 scoring options of the week in Jonathan Taylor and Justin Herbert. They are far and away the championship favorites at this point in the season.

Tough loss for Shivang - this is only his 2nd time being a top-3 weekly scorer but unfortunately had to run into the Sitaram juggernaut. It was a solid week all around, but he Wentz and Hollywood Brown were the only players that had standout weeks which simply isn’t enough against this caliber of an opponent. He can’t be too upset though - despite only scoring above league average 4 times this year, he’s still only 1 game out of 5th seed leaving the door wide open for a playoff run.

Aman (85.94) [5-4] vs Akshay (81.14) [4-5]
This was an enormous win for Aman who picked up a win despite scoring the 4th-least points of the week. This not only pushes Aman to 5-4, but it also takes away a win from someone who he’s in a fierce competition with for a playoff spot. There were underperformances all around his roster, but Jimmy G and Jettas did just enough in Brady and Evans’ absences to secure Aman the dub. Barely clinging on to a playoff spot at the moment, Aman will still need to rattle off at least another 2 wins over the rest of the season to secure a playoff berth.

Brutal, brutal loss for Akshay who’s now dropped 5 in a row driven primarily by Mahomes doing his best Sam Darnold impression. A 15 point performance from Mahomes would’ve been enough here, but he couldn’t even muster that. While the first 2 losses of his losing streak were due to tough competition, there’s absolutely no excuse for the eggs that he’s lain the last 3 weeks as the expected wins of his opponents over that stretch have been 1, 3, and 3. Being at Anish’s level and averaging 90 points per game over the last 3 weeks would’ve been enough to power Akshay to a 7-2 record, but here we are. After sitting at the top of the league after 4 weeks, Akshay will be fighting for his life not only for a playoff berth, but also to escape a spot in the toilet bowl where he surely has PTSD from finishing dead last in the league for the last 2 years running.

The playoff picture is quite interesting at the moment. We’ve got two teams that are virtual locks (Sitarams and Madurais), two teams that would need a pretty significant collapse to miss the playoffs (Methis and Jeffrey), and 5 teams who will likely be fighting for the last two spots and who are only separated by 2 games. Bring out the popcorn everyone, this should make for an exciting finish.

Reminder about the structure of the last 3 weeks of the regular season + playoffs:

There will be a lottery each of the last 3 weeks to determine matchups. The #1 seed will get first dibs to pick their opponent. For week 14, there’ll be one additional restriction in that you can only pick an opponent who is within 2 seeds of your current seed.

In the 2nd round of the playoffs, the matchups will be reseeded ensuring that the #1 seed plays the lower seeded team that advances to the semifinals.

Anant vs Akshay - Probably the most exciting matchup of the week, we’ve got two teams that are just on the outside looking in for a playoff spot where one team will emerge with a .500 record and one team will be 2 games below .500. Don’t think Anant imagined starting Brandon Bolden, D’Ernest Johnson, and Pat Friermuth at the start of the season, but he’ll need big games from them to defeat a very solid group from Akshay including Najee Harris and De’Andre Swift. I’m thinking big weeks from Mike Williams and Patrick Mahomes will not only silence the haters but will carry Akshay to a win in a matchup that could come down to Akshay’s kicker on Monday night.

Kishan vs Jeffrey - Will Kishan play an active kicker / defense? Will Jeffrey find an RB pairing that’ll score over 10 points combined? Those are the crucial questions of this matchup which in all likelihood doesn’t mean that much for either team. Jeffrey’s in a great spot for the playoffs regardless, and Kishan’s just playing for pride. If Kishan plays a starting kicker and defense, I think he’ll win this one, but that’s a big if.
