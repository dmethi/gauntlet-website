Another week, another win for the Vikings who look like a completely transformed team from the team that lost to the Falcons just a few weeks ago. With a few more wins, they’ll be in the thick of the playoff race which seemed unthinkable a couple weeks ago when they were sitting at 1-5.

Just like the playoff race is heating up in the NFL, it’s also heating up in our league as we saw 2 more teams essentially punch their ticket into their playoffs with 2 more teams being eliminated.

Methis (106.90) [4-6] vs Aman (118.26) [6-4]
Another painful L in the books for the Methis, and the only thought that was going through my mind was why the fuck do I keep subjecting myself to the pain of fantasy football year after year.

This was an admittedly solid week for Aman, posting the 2nd highest score of the week. Now a winner of 3 of the last 4 weeks, his surging team has put himself in a great position to make the playoffs after a disappointing season last year. Even though Davante Adams had a quiet week by his standards, Ronald Jones more than made up for it with an unreal 98 yard touchdown en route to a 25 point performance. In the end, the player that really did me in was Justin Jefferson - his dominant 8 catch, 135 yard performance on Monday night was the nail in the coffin for this matchup. In all honesty though, there’s no other player I’d rather have rail my team. To secure his playoff spot, Aman essentially needs only one more win in the next three weeks, and he’ll hope it comes next week against Hunter.

Humiliating loss for us as my lineup decisions once again cost us a victory for the 3rd straight week. 3 weeks ago, my last minute decision to play Jimmy Garoppolo over Derek Carr cost us a win against the Madurais. 2 weeks ago, my last minute decision to play Adam Thielen over Giovanni Bernard cost us a win against Shivang. Last week, my last minute decision to play Giovanni Bernard over Adam Thielen cost us a win against Aman. In another world, we could be 7-3 with the 2nd-highest points scored in the league, but instead we’re 4-6 and in need for a miracle run to make the playoffs. Absolutely humiliating stuff that’s forced me to step down from my position as offensive coordinator for our team - I’m ceding all lineup decisions to Krish.

Much needed cakewalk win for the Sitarams as they picked up their first dub in 4 weeks. Josh Allen was the headline with his 26 points (and insane touchdown to Diggs near the end of the game), but an extremely underrated play was Salvon Ahmed, an absolute waiver wire scrub that they plugged in to their lineup just a few minutes before the Dolphins game. 200 IQ move. Even with lingering questions at their RB positions, the Sitarams have put themselves in a good spot to make a late season charge but will still need to win 2 out of their next 3 games to qualify. Absolutely disgusting performance by Jeffrey (2nd lowest score of the season in the league), and this essentially signals that he has given up on his season. The real task that’s left to all of us is figuring out an enforceable punishment for Jeffrey that he won’t weasel out of, as right now he’s looking as a prime contender for the toilet bowl champion. It’s actually ridiculous how competitive the league is this year. After 10 weeks, only 2 games separate the 1st place team from the 7th place team. Every team has at least 2 wins and 3 losses. The first and last place teams are separated only by roughly 20 points per week in scoring (which, in all honesty, is not THAT much). No one’s running away with it and almost everyone is still in the running with only 3 weeks left in the regular season.

There was only one tight end this week that scored over 10 points, and only 3 that scored over 6 points. This position is a wasteland, and I’m firmly in favor of replacing the TE position on fantasy rosters with a WR/TE slot next season so that we don’t have to keep wasting roster spots on players whom on a good week will have like 2 catches for 40 yards.

This is the first time all season that we had 2 teams whose top-2 running backs combined for over 50 points - Hunter and Ishan. Both of them are averaging significantly over league average in terms of RB scoring, and Hunter is actually scoring 11 points per week greater than league average from his RBs. Given that the difference between the top scoring team and bottom scoring team is 20 points per week, this is an ENORMOUS edge and it’s frankly shocking that it hasn’t translated to more wins.

Most losses when scoring above league average: Hunter, with 4. Most wins when scoring below league average: Madurais, with 4 (no one else has more than 2).

Most difficult schedule the last 4 weeks: Me and Hunter - both of us have played 3 teams who’ve scored top-3 in the week. Easiest schedule the last 4 weeks: Madurais and Akshay - neither has played a team that has scored in the top half of league scoring each week, and the Madurais played 2 teams who scored the lowest in the week.
