Woke up on Friday morning with no memory of the last twelve hours, a flight to catch in 90 minutes, a 17 point differential mine and Jeffrey’s kickers from Thursday night, and Jeffrey in my DMs asking me to publish the weekly report where I’ll be forced to begrudgingly praise his fucking team. Yeah, I was down bad. But hey, we’ve got a job to do, and here we go.

Methis (90.22) [7-3] vs Varun (81.52) [5-5]
Dicey, dicey game that saw Krish and I clenching until the dying seconds of Monday Night Football. In the end, we somehow came out with the win despite scoring the 4th-least points in the week, our worst performance in a winning effort. But hey, we’ll take it, and this was a massive week for us which virtually locks us for a playoff spot. We only had 3 starters score in double digits, but that’s okay when you’ve got a healthy CMC on your team. In the 3 games he’s been fully healthy, he’s had 20 points each week. My guy. Lots of warning signs from our performance for the week, but we’ll chalk it up to a 1 week hangover with our team mourning from the loss of Bobby Trees.

Real tough loss for Varun who not only falls back to .500 but falls out of the playoff picture as it currently stands. He simply was unable to make up for the late loss of Nick Chubb — his RBs combined for only 13 points this week, their lowest output by more than 10 all year. Clearly heading into this week, he wasn’t expecting Hunter Renfrow to be his second highest scoring player for the week, that’s certainly not going to be a recipe for success moving forward. He’ll hope that a more meaningful role for OBJ and the return of Nick Chubb will provide the ammunition he needs to mount a successful final playoff push.

Madurais (73.98) [7-3] vs Aman (91.70) [6-4]
The concerns continue to mount for the Madurais whose scoring has simply cratered since the loss of Derrick Henry. After averaging 120 points per week the first 8 weeks of the season, they’ve only averaged 80 points per week the last 2 weeks. Yes they’ve essentially got a playoff spot, but now the issue becomes finding the right roster of players around Davante and Zeke to be a serious threat in the playoffs. They’ve got 4 weeks to figure it out because right now, it’s not looking pretty.

Big time dub for Aman who took one massive step forward towards locking up a playoff spot. It’s always feast or famine for him given his reliance on the Bucs and the Vikings, but this week it certainly paid off with his Brady / Evans / Cook / Jettas tandem combining for 64 points, more than making up for the massive donut that Gesicki put up. Despite failing to clear 110 points in any of his last 5 weeks, Aman has won 3 games during that stretch separating himself from the group of 4-5 teams that are fiercely fighting for the last 2 playoff spots.

Sitarams (111.80) [8-2] vs Anish (105.34) [3-7]
Another week, another real solid performance from the Sitarams who are on an unbelievable roll, currently riding a 5 game win streak. They’ve got the #1 RB and #1 WR in the game at this point, and their 3-headed monster of Taylor / Fournette / JRob looks unstoppable. Since Week 3, they’ve had at least 25 points from their running backs in every single week. Kupp did give them a bit of a scare this week waiting till the 4th quarter of Monday night to secure the dub for them, but regardless of the outcome of this game the Sitarams have proven yet again that their consistency is unparalleled, scoring above league average for the 10th straight time this year. We’ve all been waiting all year for the cracks to show in this team, and I guess we’ll have to wait another week.

Brutal luck for Anish who lost despite posting his highest expected win total of the year. Believe it or not, this is the first week that Anish has scored above league average, scoring his highest point total for the year in the process. In the end it was all for naught. Very solid all around effort for Anish, with 6 players scoring above 10 points. It appears that his roster is rounding into form right when his playoff hopes are on the verge of extinguishing. Painful stuff. Believe it or not, Anish is 2-3 in games in which he’s bottom-2 in league scoring, and 1-4 in games in which he isn’t. Maybe the recipe for him making a miracle playoff run is going to be positioning himself within the league cellar and letting his stellar fantasy defense do the work.

Shivang (71.10) [4-6] vs Ishan (104.12) [3-7]
After battling back from 2-4 to 4-4 on the year, Shivang has given away all that hard work with 2 crushing back-to-back losses. Now at 4-6, his back is certainly against the wall in his campaign to make the playoffs. Simply put, barely anyone showed up to play in this one for Shivang. Kelce and Devonta Smith combined for literally half of his points, no one else scoring more than 7. This is now the 5th time this year Shivang’s quarterback has failed to clear 10 points. Failing to clear 100 points for the 4th time in 5 weeks, it appears that the wheels are finally falling off for Shivang’s patchwork team that has long been amongst the lowest scoring teams in the league.

Is there a glimmer of hope for the man? After dealing with disappointment after disappointment all year, Ishan’s studs finally showed up to play. Diggs and Gibson had long-awaited breakout games, combining for 46 points and providing more than enough ammo to give Ishan his 3rd win of the year. Somehow, this is only the 2nd time that Ishan has faced an opponent who has scored less than 100 points. Maybe this week marks a turning of the tides in terms of luck for Ishan as the door is not closed yet on a playoff run. In any case, he can rest easy finally getting confirmation that the players he drafted weren’t simply washed or bad picks. They just took a second to get going.

Kishan (99.94) [1-9] vs Jeffrey 👑 (121.28) [7-3]
Good bounceback week for Kishan recovering from his disgusting 43-point showing a week earlier, but as has been the norm all year, he fails to get any matchup luck facing the highest scoring team of the week for the second time this year. Not a whole lot to say about his performance, his studs Josh Allen and Tyreek Hill played well (with the exception of Aaron Jones kinda), just not enough support anywhere else. The epitome of this matchup was when Mahomes missed a wide open Tyreek on a 50-yard TD only to find Darrel fucking Williams, Jeffrey’s guy, on a 40-yard deep ball where he mossed his defender. That’s the 22 point swing in the matchup right there and imo is a great representation of how this season has gone for Kishan. Tough luck my man.

After falling down to 7th in the power rankings after Week 7, Jeffrey’s made a phenomenal run over the last 3 games to jump to 2nd in the power rankings and 7-3 on the year, all of a sudden looking like one of the most formidable teams in the league. He’s doing this DESPITE getting only 14 points from his QBs the last 3 weeks COMBINED. It remains to be seen how sustainable this strategy is moving forward but can’t argue with the results right now that have brought Jeffrey screaming up the league rankings. Jeffrey may have a point that he has a knack of hitting on the sleepers with Darrel Williams and Rhamondre Stevenson combining for 50 points. This is the look of Jeffrey’s team without Mixon, Saquon, Kyler, and Julio. Yikes.

Anant (92.54) [4-6] vs Akshay (113.64) [5-5]
A massive matchup pitting two 4-5 teams in desperate need of a win against each other, Anant comes up holding the short end of the stick as a donut from TJ Hockenson and a horrific performance from Russel Wilson took away from what was otherwise a pretty solid performance. CeeDee, D’Ernest Johnson, and Mark Ingram all feasted, combining for 61 points, but special teams also made a big difference as Anant was outscored by 22 points in that department which ended up being the difference in the game. Anant’s team is simply going in the wrong direction heading into a must-win matchup next week.

HUGE win for Akshay, snapping a 5-game losing streak. It’s only fitting that this came on the back of Mahomes’ monster performance proving to the world not only that he is the best QB in the league but also that Akshay’s team is back on the map. With 36 points from his superstar QB, 24 points from his defense, and 30 points from his star RB duo, Akshay’s sending the message that he doesn’t need WR scoring, although he’ll need more scoring from that position if he wants to mount a serious run. In any case, the monkey is finally off the back, and Akshay is firmly back in playoff contention.
