With only 20 points less than the top scorer in the league on the season, securing a playoff spot really shouldn’t be this stressful, but hey that’s just the unpredictability of fantasy football. Our bid for playoff contention will continue next week as we take on Hunter.

Brutal week for Varun, and his lowest scoring week since week 5 came at a brutal time as he’s now BARELY holding on to a playoff spot. Everything went wrong for him this week (only 4 players scoring in double digits), but the most embarrassing stat of all is that his kicker and defense combined for -1 points. Here’s Varun’s current pattern of wins and losses on the season: L2, W3, L2, W3, L1. That pattern would seem to indicate that he’s on track to lose next week against Aman. He’ll hope the return of Raheem Mostert will help to buck the trend, and he absolutely needs the win next week to avoid going into a must-win Week 13 matchup against Anant.

Sitarams (109.96) [6-5] vs Anant (89.50) [7-4]

Ishan (108.94) [8-3] vs Jeffrey (108.84) [3-8]

Tough, tough loss for Aman - on the verge of clinching playoffs, he came away with a heartbreaking loss that now throws him into the fray of teams at 6-5. He’ll now need one win in the next 2 to qualify, and he now has matchups against Varun and Jeffrey on the slate.

Proposed rule changes
Looking ahead to next season, based on what’s happened this season, here are a couple rule changes I have in mind:

6th seed by points - top 5 seeds get in by record, 6th seed gets decided by whoever has the most points out of the non-playoff teams. Would give players who’ve gotten dicked by their schedule a chance for playoffs, and would make people more engaged since no one in theory would be eliminated until the very end. Would also still keep the inherent unpredictability of weekly matchups since top 5 teams still get in by their record.

TE position replaced by W/T - the tight end position is such a wasteland this year, would probably be more fun to have the option of starting a WR instead - would make rosters more deep and probably increase weekly scoring.

Decimal scoring for kickers - don’t understand why this isn’t already the case, but essentially a 29 yard field goal would be worth 2.9. A 1 point swing between a 49 and 50 yard field goal is so arbitrary.
