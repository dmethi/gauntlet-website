Madurais 👑 (134.36) [7-1] vs Varun (96.32) [4-4]

Labeled a top-heavy team all season long, it’s unbelievably impressive that they were able to outscore the next highest scoring team in the league by 23 points with Davante sitting for COVID and Zeke/Henry combining for less than 15 points. They were, however, dealt a huge body blow this week with the news that Henry will likely be out for the rest of the regular season. Their depth will certainly be put to the test, but if this week is any indication, the Madurais are well prepared to weather the storm even if their path to a championship has grown significantly more difficult.

Tough loss for Varun dropping him back to .500 on the year. The Lamar bye week was brutal, with Teddy only giving Varun 12 points. The return of Nick Chubb significantly underwhelmed as he was only able to muster 7 points on 17 touches. While Darrel Henderson and DK Metcalf were bright spots, Varun’s WR depth is starting to become a serious issue, especially with the news that OBJ will likely not play another snap with the Cleveland Browns. Scoring below league average for 3 straight weeks and sitting 2nd to last in the league at wide receiver scoring, Varun will need to find a solution at WR2 if he wants to break out of the middle of the pack and make a playoff push. Is Hunter Renfrow the answer? We’ll soon find out.

In any case, Aman still has some solid firepower on his team, and if Dalvin/JJ can return to form, he has as good a chance as any of the other 4-4 teams to break out and secure a playoff spot.

Odds and Ends
Akshay - The last two weeks are the first two weeks this year that Akshay has scored below league average. After hitting #1 in the league after week 4, Akshay’s been on a steady trend downwards, now falling to to #6. It’s even worse when you look at his power ranking - he peaked in Week 1 with a power ranking of #3 and that’s taken a precipitous fall the last few weeks dropping him all the way to #9. A large part of that is due to declining WR production - his WRs combined for over 30 points 3 out of the first 4 weeks, and only 1 time in the last 4 weeks.

Jeffrey - He’s been alternating wins and losses since Week 2, which puts him on track for a loss this week. Let’s see if the pattern holds up. His team’s winning formula has changed dramatically from the early part of the season. In the first 4 weeks of the season, his QB was averaging 28 points per game and his WRs were averaging 38.5 points per game, combined. Those have now dropped to 14.73 and 27.5 respectively. His points per game haven’t dropped though - he’s made up for that drop in production by substantially improved RB performance (24.5 to 38.1) and K performance (4.7 to 11.5).

Anish - Hard to point to a single culprit for Anish’s underperformance as he’s bottom half in the league in scoring at every single position, but his biggest achilles heel has been his special teams. He’s never gotten more than 6 points from his kicker, averaging 4.5 points per week. Outside of a 19 point week during week 2, his defense has failed to score more than 4 points, averaging 3.8 points per week, dropping to 1.7 points per week if you remove the outlier 19-point performance. His shocking ineptitude has accelerated as of late - he’s been bottom-2 in scoring for 3 of the last 4 weeks, and this is primarily due to his WR production dropping from 36 points per game over the first 4 weeks to 23 points per week.

Anant - Anant has seen the full spectrum in terms of rankings this year. He opened the year at #2 in the power rankings and sleeper rankings, fell as low as #11 in the sleeper rankings and #12 in the power rankings, but has worked his way back up now to #5 in the sleeper rankings and #3 in the power rankings. Real story of redemption there. He’s consistently had a solid WR group this year, but they’ve really turned it on as of late - his WR crew is averaging an absurd 51 points per game over the last 4 weeks. This has significantly helped mask the weakness of his RB group which has combined for over 25 points just once on the year.

Aman - Story of the season for Aman so far has been tough matchup after tough matchup. He has the 2nd highest points against in the league, and only once this year has his opponent not been top-5 in scoring for the week, so he’s really had to earn his wins. Specifically, Aman’s opponent’s QBs and RBs are averaging 10 points per game more than league average combined. Luckily for him, he’s peaked at the right times during his difficult schedule - in his losses, he’s losing by an average of 35 points, and in his wins, he’s winning by an average of only 15 points. Basically, he’s laying an egg in weeks that he likely would’ve lost anyways, and putting up solid performances when his team has a better chance of winning. Nice.

Ishan - Like Aman, Ishan’s had a really difficult schedule thus far into the season. In the last 5 weeks, his opponents have all scored at least 110 points - he’s the only person in the league that can say that. Granted, he hasn’t made things easy on himself (he’s scored below league average 4 of the last 5 weeks), but it’s been tough sledding regardless. Even though he’s finally got a solution to his QB problem the last two weeks, his points per game haven’t improved as a result of his WR production bottoming out (combining for only 42 points the last two weeks). If he wants to climb out of this hole, he’ll have to figure out a way to get his team to all show up every week.

Kishan - What’s more to say for Kishan, just so so much potential wasted. His special teams issues have already been documented, but an equally large (if not bigger) concern for him is his awful TE production. From weeks 3 to 7, he received a TOTAL of 7 points from that position, and has only got more than 6 points from that position twice. Might want to consider hitting Aman up for Mike Gesicki.

Shivang - While we’ve just touched on a few people who have been getting shafted by their schedules, Shivang is someone who has been getting consistently bailed out. He’s only faced an opponent who’s scored over 110 points twice this year, and his opponent’s average expected wins is 3.8. He’s faced a bottom-3 team 4 times this year and actually managed to lose two of those games. There are several holes on his roster, but the biggest among them is his QB. While the league average QB score is 20 points per week, he’s only had a QB score over 20 points two times this year. There have been 4 weeks where his QB has scored less than 10 points. The rest of the league has 8 such weeks combined.

Varun - Interesting season so far for Varun. He’s 4th in the league in QB scoring, but that rank masks the inconsistency that he’s got from that position - he’s scored less than 20 points at that position 5 different times this year. The reason that he’s got 4 wins this year is his rock solid RB group - they haven’t scored less than 23 points in a single week this year. Given his weakness at WR (2nd to last in production at that position), he’ll need his RB core to continue to carry him in order to make a serious playoff push.

Sitarams - Not many more superlatives to be said for the Sitarams sensational performance 8 weeks through the season. They’ve yet to score below league average in a week, and are scoring at or above average at every single position besides QB. Over the last 6 weeks, they’ve been averaging a combined 82 points from their RB and WR group. Even if they cool off at those positions, they have a lot of room to pick up the slack - they haven’t had a QB score over 20 or a TE score over 10 in any of the last 4 weeks. Considering they’ve got Herbert and Darren Waller manning those positions, I’d expect that to change quickly.

Madurais - It’s hard to overstate how dominant the Madurais RBs have been, but the most eye popping stat is that their RBs have combined for over 40 points in 6 of the last 8 weeks. That’s INSANE. And despite not spending pretty much any draft capital at the TE position, they currently have the 4th most points at that position with the combination of Dawson Knox and CJ Uzomah. Much respect to their roster management.

Methis - This past week was the 5th time we’ve finished in the top-3 in league scoring, tied with the Madurais for league lead in that stat. We’ve been able to do this despite being dead last in WR scoring on the season. Granted part of this is because we’ve been starting Cordarelle Patterson in our WR slot and he’s been labeled as a RB for these stats. But outside that position, we’ve been above average everywhere else. We’ve yet to have a week where we scored less than 30 points from our WR group, and only twice have we got less than 20 points from our QB. We’ve also got 9 points or more from our defense 5 different times this year. With this kind of production at those positions, we’ve got a pretty high floor and that’s shown since we’ve only scored less than 105 points once all year. Will the return of McCaffrey catapult our already-solid lineup to championship status? We’ll have to see.
