Absolutely shameful performances.

Sitarams [90.96] (4-4) vs Aman [98.82] (5-3)
Aman just managed to slide by in this matchup on the backs of Davante Adams and his special teams who combined to score almost 60 points. Despite Lamar Jackson and Kareem Hunt combining for under 20 points (they were both ranked as top-5 plays for the week at their respective positions!!), he secured the dub and is quietly on a roll now - he’s won 4 of his last 5 games. His team did suffer a big blow as Kittle is likely out for the year. Another tough week for the Sitarams as they now slide to 4-4 after losing 2 weeks in a row. This marks the 3rd week in a row that they’ve failed to hit the 100-point threshold, and they’ve now scored below league average in 6 out of 8 weeks this season.

The main issue has been their QB - this marks the 4th week in a row that their starting QB has failed to put up more than 16 points, and you’ve got to wonder if Josh Allen’s early season magic is running out. It certainly doesn’t help that they just lost Myles Gaskin, a breakout RB. They’ll quickly need to turn their season around or else they risk sliding into irrelevance like Akshay, and they’ve got the opportunity to start next week in their matchup against Varun.

Varun [119.54] (4-4) vs Ishan [107.14] (5-3)
Just like everything seemingly went wrong for Varun last week, everything went right for him this week as he posted his highest total since Week 4. You’re certainly in a good spot when Nyheim Hines and the Eagles Defense combine for 40 points, and Patty Mahomes’ 36 points were just the cherry on top. With Chase Edmonds coming off bye and the imminent returns of Allan Lazard and Nick Chubb, Varun’s team is trending upwards and has a lot of momentum going into next week’s matchup against the Sitarams.

Tough loss for Ishan as this marks the third time he’s faced someone that’s been top-3 in scoring for the week. In his 3 losses on the season, his opponents have averaged 135 points. Bye weeks and injuries derailed an otherwise solid week for him as his fill ins for Chris Carson and Terry McLaurin combined for only 8 points. Either way, he’s sitting in a good spot at 5-3 and will look to get back into the win column next week against Anant.

Shivang [132.94] (6-2) vs Hunter [109.76] (2-6)
Dominant bounce-back performance for Shivang as he proved he could win without elite performances from his defense and kicker. Russel Wilson, Robert Woods, and Travis Kelce each scored over 20 points and Aiyuk chipped in with 19. Victory was never really in doubt for Shivang as he comfortably retakes his spot atop the league power rankings and standings. He’ll look to keep it rolling next week against yours truly. I’m running out of superlatives to describe the bad luck that Hunter has had this year as his losing streak now extends to 6 straight games. Over the last 6 weeks, he has yet to face an opponent that has scored in the bottom half of the league for the week, including 3 weeks where he has faced someone who’s either scored the 2nd highest or the highest weekly total, and that trend continued this week as Shivang had the highest weekly total. Despite scoring the 5th most points this year, he’s tied for second-to-last in the standings. Let’s just say I wouldn’t stand next to Hunter in a lightning storm.

His opponent for next week, the Madurais, ironically have had the exact opposite seasonal trend as Hunter. While Hunter won his first two games and lost the next six, the Madurais lost the first two and are currently winners of six straight. Hunter will hope to break both his own losing streak and the Madurais winning streak next week in a bid to keep his slim but fading playoff hopes alive.

Kishan [128.28] (3-5) vs Jeffrey [103.72] (2-6)
Where the hell did this come from?? After 4 weeks in a row in which he failed to reach 90 points, Kishan snapped his 4-game losing streak in impressive fashion as he posted the 2nd-highest total of the week, reminding the league that he isn’t simply a cakewalk.

season 4 showtime GIF by Billions
Despite DeAndre Hopkins being on bye, Dalvin Cook’s 47 points propelled Kishan to his first respectable showing in weeks. His team looks revitalized, and his core of Cook, Julio, and Hopkins remains probably the most dangerous 3-man group in the league. He’ll look to build upon this sensational performance next week against Anant. Another disappointing week for Jeffrey as this marks the FIFTH time this season that he has faced an opponent with a top-3 weekly total (and third time in the last four weeks). DK Metcalf was the lone bright spot in a week where only 4 of his starters scored over 10 points. With Michael Thomas returning next week, Jeffrey will hope the dynamic duo of Metcalf and Thomas will be the spark he needs to start a late playoff push, and the first step is taking care of next week’s matchup against Akshay.

Anant [95.66] (6-2) vs Akshay [67.62] (1-7)
Anant will certainly take the dub on a week where his highest scoring player was Zach Moss. Given Akshay’s putrid total, this was a good week for his studs to have (relatively) quiet performances. Anant has now won his last 3 games and now adds both Aaron Jones (who was out for a couple games with injury) and Christian McCaffrey to an already-dangerous lineup. He might not lose another game this year, and he’ll look to keep the win streak going next week against Kishan. I really, REALLY had hoped that last week marked Akshay’s breakout and that he had put the days of him being a bottom-feeder behind him. I was wrong, and this week he proved without a doubt that he belongs at the bottom of the league as this was his 7th time scoring in the bottom-3 in weekly scoring, setting a season-low for points scored for himself in the process.

This week was the 2nd-lowest scoring week of the year — only 6 teams cleared 100 points, and 3 teams failed to clear 70 points.

Hunter and Jeffrey have both had ridiculously unlucky schedules. Based on their expected win percentage, their difficult schedule has cost them 2 wins as both in theory should be 4-4. On the other hand, the Madurais have had the easiest/luckiest schedule, as based on their expected win percentage, their easy schedule has gifted them 2 wins more than they deserve. Here’s a stat Hunter will surely enjoy - Hunter has a higher expected win percentage than the Madurais. Hunter is 2-6, and the Madurais are 6-2.

Shivang has the highest season scoring from QB, TE, K, and DEF, but he’s dead last in RB scoring. If he had even decent RBs, he’d be borderline unstoppable.

So far this season, WRs have scored roughly the same amount per week as RBs. Are RBs really worth that much more than WRs? Who knows.
