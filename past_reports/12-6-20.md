Playoffs Scenarios
Madurais

As the only 9-3 team in the league and with Shivang and Ishan facing each other this week, the Madurais are locked into a first-round bye (since there can be at most 1 other 9-win team in the league). The only question for them is if they get the first or second seed. A win locks them into the first seed, while a loss would almost certainly bump them to second seed (since the winner of Ishan/Shivang’s matchup would win the tiebreaker based on points scored - each of them have around 70 more points scored on the season than the Madurais).

Ishan/Shivang

Both of these teams are sitting in nearly identical positions - 8-4 record with ~1350 points scored on the season. In almost poetic fashion, they happen to be facing each other this week. The winner of this matchup is all but guaranteed the 1st seed in the playoffs, while the loser could in theory slip as low as the 6th seed. The loser will be at 8-5 on the season, and based on how many of the 7-5 teams win their matchups, they could potentially lose the point scored tiebreaker. For example, if Anant wins and outscores the loser of this matchup by 25 points, he’d take the 3rd seed and the loser of this matchup would drop to 4th. There is one way for both these teams to guarantee 2nd and 3rd seed - if they both bench all of their players so that their matchup results in a tie. In that case, they’d both finish at 8-4-1, but since Ishan has 3.5 more points scored on the season than Shivang, he’d end up getting the bye. I doubt Shivang would let this happen lol, but an interesting scenario to consider regardless.

Anant/Sitarams/Aman

All of these teams find themselves in win-and-in situations. Each of them could finish as high as the 2nd seed or as low as the 8th seed, an unreal amount of variance given that there’s only 1 more week left in the season. However, all of their playoff positions are in a precarious spot. Since the Methis are 6-6 but have more points scored than each of these teams, if any one of them loses and the Methis win, they’d almost certainly be overtaken in the standings based on points scored. The person who’s spot is most in jeopardy is Aman - with the 3rd lowest points scored on the season, if he loses, he’s almost certainly out.

Varun/Methis

Both of these teams are currently the first ones out of the playoffs. The Methis have a much better shot at playoffs based on their points scored - a win paired with a loss from any of Anant/Sitarams/Aman all but guarantees a playoffs spot. The matchups are also aligned in their favor - the Methis are facing Kishan (coming off a 48 point performance) while Anant/Sitarams/Aman each have more difficult matchups, facing Varun/Madurais/Jeffrey respectively. Varun has a much more difficult path to the playoffs. Since he currently only has more points scored than Aman, his most plausible path to the playoffs is a win against Anant, Aman losing to Jeffrey, and the Methis losing to Kishan since that would put him in at 6th seed over Aman. For him to overtake Anant/Sitarams or the Methis if they win against Kishan, he’d need an absolutely monstrous performance.
