Despite the loss and ensuing panic from Ishan, this is a relatively encouraging start. Last year, he only cleared 110 points 3 total times during the regular season. Things are looking a little brighter this year. Perhaps a return to the playoffs are in store? Only time will tell.

Plaxico Burress’s Handguns
1. Saquonda Forever (Methis) [1-0]

Panic level: 4/10

Behind a commanding Week 1 victory led by our superstar duo, there are some definite cracks in the foundation. ARob and Pitts, 2 players we were counting on to be set-in-stone starters (we expected Pitts to be elite tbh) combined for only 4 points. Trey Lance looked rough. We’ve got some time though. It looks like Saquon, Jettas, Dillon, and Amon-Ra are all smash picks, which gives us a good deal of cushion to figure out the rest of our lineup.

2. P. Sherman Wallerby Way (Madurais) [1-0]

Panic level: 3/10

If week 1 gives any indication, this team has tons of real solid pieces to build off of. Mahomes looks like a criminal steal, and Clyde/Cook look like a pretty formidable RB duo.

There are some definite warning signs around CeeDee (one of their top picks), but their bench depth and star power give them the luxury of patience. Oh, and they’ll also soon have JK Dobbins back to full health, which should be a big plus.

3. Next Year (Ishan) [0-1]

Panic level: 2/10

Tough loss no doubt, but this week should alleviate a lot stress from Ishan. Swift, Fournette, and Diggs is as formidable a 3-man combo as any in the league (all should challenge for top-5 at their respective positions), and there were no real busts anywhere on their roster (besides Dak).

Better days are sure to be ahead.

4. 2 girls, 1 Kupp (Akshay) [0-1]

Panic level: 4/10

Obviously not the best performance, but underneath the disappointment, there’s a lot of promise here. The emergence of Gabriel Davis and James Robinson easily offset the relative disappointments of Mike Williams and Breece Hall (who didn’t even look that bad). Cooper Kupp is Cooper Kupp, Antonio Gibson looks like a steal, and the Steelers combo of Najee/Freiermuth should be solid at worst.

No reason to hammer the panic button here.

CMC’s usage in a real shitty, Baker-led offense

McLaurin only catching 2 passes and facing tons of target competition

3. Scboom5 (Shivang) [0-1]

Panic level: 7/10

The concerning: Lamar isn’t running as much which might cap his ceiling, Ekeler isn’t catching as many passes, and Shivang doesn’t have a real flex player on his roster right now

Overall, Kelce was such a smash pick that Shivang will have a chance in every matchup, but there are definitely some big question marks on his roster.

The ceiling looks to be pretty capped for Aaron Jones and Diontae, and Pollard looks like he may struggle to be a top-30 RB.
