Akshay (123.58) [1-0] vs Sitarams (122.68) [0-1]
Line: O 220.6, Akshay covered +14.2

STUNNING underdog effort from Akshay as he came out of literally nowhere and put up the 3rd highest score of the week. Last year, it took until Week 7 for Akshay to break 105 points, but he’s already broken that barrier this season and looks to be off to the races. This is despite the fact that his 3 most expensive players from his auction draft who cost over $130 (JK Dobbins, DeAndre Swift, and Najee Harris) contributed less than 6 points to his starting lineup. Akshay was still able to find a way to put points on the board, led by productive weeks from his superstar Patrick Mahomes, rookie Ty’Son Williams, and has-beens Mike Williams and Brandin Cooks. I’m tempted to say this was a complete fluke, but Akshay also had 3 players on his bench put up 20 points. Maybe we shit talked this man so much to the point that his team is actually…not bad? Only time will tell, but for now, Akshay has regained at least a shred of his long-lost dignity and self respect as, for the first time in over 2 years, he sits with a win percentage of over .250 (deadass!)

Devastating defeat for the Sitarams. Despite disappointing weeks from James Robinson, Justin Herbert, and Terry McLaurin, the Sitarams still had a great chance to win on Monday night thanks to the Herculean efforts of Cooper Kupp and Adam Thielen. They simply needed Darren Waller to outscore Ty’Son Williams by 6 points, and he was oh so close. After getting peppered with a whopping 19 targets over the course of the game, the Sitarams were just 1 5-yard catch away from sealing the win, but the Raiders decided to not target Waller over the last two drives of their game. It ended up working out for the Raiders, but it certainly didn’t work out for the Sitarams. Luckily for the Sitarams this is only week 1 and they have lots of time to recover.

Jeffrey [107.66] (1-0) vs Varun [104.80] (0-1)
Line: U 224.4, Varun covered +9.4

Another THRILLING matchup decided on Monday night, as Jeffrey was just able to squeeze out a win despite Varun’s comeback effort. Jeffrey was quite top-heavy in this matchup, as his superstars Kyler and Mixon combined for over 60% of his points, supported by solid weeks from Diontae Johnson and Tee Higgins. It worked out for him in this matchup, but it definitely is a potential cause for concern moving forward. Another cause of concern is the shockingly bad performance by Saquon Barkley, which is more of a reflection of his poor offensive line than his ability. But either way, Jeffrey secured the dub, and, with his star power, should have better weeks ahead.

Heartbreaker for Varun who was in a commanding position heading into Monday night. Thanks to Chubb, Henderson, and DK Metcalf, Varun only needed 20 points from Lamar Jackson, and considering Lamar’s matchup, this shouldn’t have been difficult. Lamar took it right down to the wire and had 19 points as the Ravens got the ball in overtime after Carr’s interception. Instead of driving down the field and getting Varun the single point he needed to win, Lamar gets strip sacked, losing 2 points on the play and crushing Varun’s hopes of victory. Tough, tough start to the season.

Madurais [95.58] (1-0) vs Ishan [89.24] (0-1)
Line: U 229.9, Madurais covered +4.3

No strangers to low scoring slugfests, the Madurais once again channeled their elite Fantasy Defense to win a matchup in which they scored under 100 points, the THIRD time they’ve done that the last 2 years. There’s no sugarcoating it, this was an absolutely ugly showing by last years runners’-up, as they only people who scored over 10 points were Tyler Lockett, their kicker Daniel Carlson, and motherfucking Cole Beasley. Either way, it doesn’t really matter how they won their matchup, they’re still 1-0 with their stars due for some serious positive regression.

This was an…interesting week for Ishan in which every single starting player scored between 5 and 13 points. This is obviously not ideal for fantasy purposes, but is still an impressive feat of consistency as his team was perfectly below average this week. Call him the Minnesota Vikings. Another roster built with the stars and scrubs approach, Ishan’s team is also due for some positive regression in future weeks, and really the only thing to take away from this matchup is that better weeks are ahead.

Anant [132.36] (1-0) vs Anish [80.92] (0-1)
Line: U 226.6, Anant covered -2.4

I suppose I should’ve seen this coming. After going in on Anant’s team after the draft, Anant reminds everyone why he’s the reigning champ by blowing Anish out of the water, scoring the second highest points in the league. This was a remarkably deep performance as well, with Anant getting solid performances out of virtually every position in his lineup and essentially ending the matchup by Sunday afternoon. Extremely impressive stuff.

In a week where Aaron Rodgers was QB35, there’s not much to do except say ‘gg’ and move on. This was certainly the furthest thing from a warm welcome that Anish might’ve expected in his return to the league, but there still are silver linings with the strong performances of Alvin Kamara and Chris Godwin. With his brutal loss, Anish also secured the #1 waiver wire pick and nabbed Elijah Mitchell, which may prove to be a bigger boost to his season than a win would have been.

Shivang [117.48] (1-0) vs Aman [91.10] (0-1)
Line: U 226.6, Shivang covered +3.4

Impressive week by Shivang who was able to put together a solid performance despite a pretty disappointing showing by his 3 receivers. He was able to do this with a sensational week from Kelce and great weeks from Jacobs, Montgomery, and TLaw. With Kelce at the helm and a clutch value picks of David Montgomery and Devonta Smith, Shivang is already locked into his sweet spot of 115-125 points of last year but will look for more upside in the coming weeks as he looks to build upon his previous season.

While Mr. Ugg Boots lived up to his billing, the rest of Aman’s roster clearly did not. Mike Evans and the two rookies Pitts and Elijah Moore all had major bust weeks, and no one else had a good enough week to compensate for those disappointing performances. This loss shook Aman so much that he already added a second manager to his team who will hopefully help steer the ship in the right direction and quickly bounce this team back from the 0-1 start.

Methis [134.76] (1-0) vs Kishan [103.00] (0-1)
Line: O 226.1, Methis covered -7.7

Wish I could say I’m surprised, but the juggernaut team that Krish and I built certainly lived up to its potential this week despite our RB2 lasting only 2 plays. Amari Cooper and the combination of Dak / Hurts at QB look to be more than enough star power to support our boy Christian McCaffrey, so my guess is our team will have a very high floor throughout the season, the only question is how high our ceiling is. After this week, it looks pretty high, but our prospects have taken a decent-sized hit with the season-ending injury to Mostert, the multi-week injury to Jeudy, and the Shanahanigans going on with Aiyuk. Even though our injured reserve is stacking up quite quickly, I know we’re gonna be just fine.

Very disappointing week for Kishan whose team put up a bit of a dud performance after a promising draft. Kishan was a victim of the shocking offensive ineptitude of the Bills and the Packers, but Aaron Jones and Josh Allen should be more than okay in the long run. There’s plenty of bright spots on this team, none greater than the superstar duo of DHop and Tyreek, and my guess is that dominant performances are in the cards for Kishan soon into the future. He’ll bounce back quickly.
