This week was an absolute roller coaster and the perfect indication of the ecstasy-inducing highs as well as the mind-crushing lows that football brings. There was a time during the week I was ready to begin construction of a temple to consecrate the coming of our one true lord and savior Amon-Ra, and there were times I was ready to swear off watching football forever.

I know I said I might need a couple of days to mentally detox from football, but who am I kidding, I’m addicted. I fucking love this sport and all the highs and lows that come with it. So we’re back with a (sooner-than-expected?) weekly recap.

This week was absolutely wild though. Some things certainly haven’t changed from years past:

Evans and Lattimore beefing

Chargers, Browns, and Raiders choking leads

Aaron Rodgers owning Chicago

Kirk Cousins being Kirk Cousins under the bright lights

But there were also some crazy surprises from this past week:

The Lions, Jags, Giants, and Jets all winning on the same day??

The Jags being the ones to shut out an opponent??

Lefty Tua throwing for 450 yards??

But there was no greater surprise than the one from our own league, where the one and only plumsmuggler99 led the league in scoring for the first time in his career. 🤯 Speechless. A redemption story for the ages.

But if that isn’t a sign of the imminent coming of the apocalypse, I don’t know what is.

We’ll get into that more deeply in a bit. For now let’s dive into the weekly recaps.

🤡 Methis (95.60) [1-1] vs Ishan (128.28) [1-1]
Yeah let’s just get this out of the way first.

Despite Trey Lance’s season-ending injury, Amon Ra’s divine showing had our matchup set up for a thrilling finish on Monday night, essentially turning into a Diggs vs Jettas head-to-head primetime matchup under the bright lights.

And as has always been the case, the Vikings player absolutely choked while the star who forced his way out absolutely shined. Jettas got locked the fuck up by Darius Slay, and Diggs showed once again why he deserves to be in the conversation for best receiver in the league, running freely through the Titans secondary. It’s only fitting that Diggs ended up outscoring Jettas by a score of 38-7.


In the end, this was simply not a matchup we deserved to win. I’ll be honest, after last week, scoring the least amount of points in the league was not my expected range of weekly outcomes, but that’s what happens when Amon-Ra is the only player on your team to score over 12 points. Shameful, shameful showing. There’s no other way to put it, this was a massive gut punch in a big game against a divisional rival. This one hurts.

For Ishan, this week once again shows that he was wildly premature in his frantic, post-week 1 panic which resulted in him changing his team name to ‘Next Year’. Don’t ask me how that seeming lack of resilience translated into a dominant bounceback performance, but it clearly struck a chord within his team’s locker room. Diggs was the obvious showstopper in this matchup, but Garbage Time Wentz™️, Swift, and Sutton all chipped in with really solid performances to propel Ishan to a comfortable margin of victory.

Miscellaneous Notes

This is Ishan’s first career dub against us as he lost both previous matchups against us in the last two years. Funny enough, he scored exactly 95 points in both matchups. This year, it was our turn to lose while putting up that exact score.

This start is eerily similar to the season start we had last year. We scored the most points in the league in week 1, followed by scoring the least points in the league in week 2. We ended up with a mediocre 7-7 record and a somewhat miraculous 3rd-place finish. Hopefully we can do a little bit better than that this year.

This is Ishan’s second consecutive year starting 1-1. Last year, he followed that up by losing 6 of his next 7 games. Don’t think it’ll take a lot for him to improve upon that colossal collapse.

Interesting note — there were same massive efficiency discrepancies between the running backs in this matchup. Saquon and Fournette combined for only 160 yards on a whopping 50 touches, while Edmunds and Swift combined for 128 yards on only 13 touches. Free our boissssssss 🙏

Madurais (96.10) [1-1] vs Akshay (118.08) [1-1]
For the second week in a row, Akshay came out to a commanding lead after Thursday night’s game with Mike Williams outscoring Patrick Mahomes. But unlike last week, there was no choke in store as Akshay was able to pick up a very comfortable and drama-free victory despite Gabe Davis’s surprise Monday night inactive. In a tale as old as time, Cooper Kupp put up a quiet 25 points, and pretty much everyone else on his team pitched in with solid if not spectacular performances (no one scored less than 8 points). Really solid comeback after last week’s disappointing loss.

Pretty gutless showing from the Madurais who never showed any grit or fight in this matchup. It started with Mahomes relatively disappointing stat line after Thursday night, and no one else from their team decided to step up and pick up the mantle - Mahomes was the only player on their team that scored over 15 points. Perhaps they spent all their emotional energy pulling out last week’s thrilling and narrow victory over Ishan.

Miscellaneous Notes

Believe it or not, this is the first time that Akshay has beat the Madurais since we’ve moved to Sleeper (2019), going 0-4 against them. 5th times the charm I guess? Big statement win for the man.

Shivang (119.52) [1-1] vs Anant (103.62) [1-1]
Unfortunately for Anant this matchup was all but finished by the time Hurts started playing on Monday night. This matchup could’ve been a thrilling QB battle between the two strongest early contenders for the #1 Fantasy QB on the year, but alas all that was left for Hurts to win back for Anant was a little shred of respectability.

For Shivang, Lamar and Bateman masked what was shaping up to be a pretty disappointing performance — no one else scored over 15. But it didn’t matter this week - the dynamic QB/WR duo combined for 61 points, over half of Shivang’s total. It wasn’t enough for the Ravens to pick up the win, but it was certainly more than enough to power Shivang to a comfortable victory, one that he’ll gladly take given Kelce’s surprising dud and Michael Pittman’s late inactive.

But you definitely have to question the level of resolve in Shivang’s locker room. Despite being up by 50 points going into Monday’s night, it took only 5 minutes of the game for Shivang to convince himself that he was going to lose. Perhaps the accumulated amount of scar tissue from Detroit’s as well as Shivang’s fantasy teams tendency to come up short in the big moments is going to hold him back this year; it looks like he’s just waiting for his season to implode. Yikes.

On the other hand, this wasn’t an awful showing by Anant. The most glaring area he came up short was in special teams — his kicker and defense combined for only 3 points. League average special teams would’ve at least made this matchup close. In the end, you have to question the upside of his team. If it requires a superhuman performance from your QB to threaten hitting 110 points, how are you going to consistently pick up wins? Only time will tell.

Miscellaneous Notes

Anant actually scored the exact same score as he did last week (down to the hundredths decimal). Don’t have the numbers on this but I don’t think I’ve ever seen this before. You can question the team’s upside, but you can’t question its consistency.

Over the last 3 years, Anant and Shivang have split their 4 head-to-head matchups, but Anant came into this matchup winning the last two. With Shivang’s victory, he takes the lead in their career head-to-heads 3-2.

Anant followed up last year’s 1-1 start by losing the next 3 games en route to a place in the toilet bowl. He’ll hope that this year goes differently.

Last year, Shivang had only 3 performances total of over 110 points. He’s already at two this year, which he hopes is a sign that this year will go better for him than last year which resulted in a spot in the toilet bowl.

Sitarams (109.26) [1-1] vs Varun (100.86) [1-1]
The phrase I would use to describe this matchup is ‘slow burner’. Literally nothing happened besides player after player putting up forgetful performance after forgetful performance. What an awful showing for both teams. The Sitarams score only looks somewhat respectable because of Aaron Jones’ monster performance, and Varun only hit 100 points because of 31 combined points from his defense and kicker.

But yuck. Not a fun matchup to track.

In the end, a win is a win, and despite the rash of concerning performances throughout their lineup, the Sitarams will be grateful to not start the season dead last in the division.

👑 Kishan (139.30) [1-1] vs Jeffrey (136.96) [0-2]
🚨 MATCHUP OF THE WEEK ALERT 🚨

In what was shaping up to be the lowest scoring matchup of the week, this one was an absolute BANGER of a matchup. Fortunately for the league and unfortunately for Jeffrey, we got to see the two highest-scoring teams of the league face off this week. We all knew the table stakes coming into this game, with Jeffrey flagrantly and visibly choosing Kishan as his #1 overall divisional pick.

I think everyone was rooting for Kishan this week. Way to prove Jeffrey wrong.

Some fascinating action on Sunday set the stage for an absolutely thrilling finish on Monday night. Nick Chubb, Tee Higgins, and the Buccaneers defense propelled Kishan to a massive total on Sunday, while Waddle, Mark Andrews, Curtis Samuel, and David Montgomery helped Jeffrey put up a huge total of his own, setting up a Monday night showdown of Henry + Goedert + Tyler Bass against Jake Elliott, with Kishan needing to overcome a ~30 point deficit. Late into Monday night’s game, Kishan took a slim 2-point lead, but with the Eagles setting up for a field goal (and a massive 4 points for Jeffrey), it looked like Kishan’s dreams of pulling off the upset victory would come up just short. Fate had other plans, and the kick ended up being BLOCKED, and that was all she wrote for this matchup.

Incredible matchup with an incredible ending.

Man I love fantasy football.

For Kishan, we won’t talk about how awful Tom Brady, Derrick Henry, and DJ Moore looked. Instead, we’ll simply celebrate a huge, statement victory for the program, one which looks to be on the precipice of finally asserting itself as a force to be reckoned with in the league after a long time in the doldrums. Inspirational stuff.

You gotta feel for Jeffrey, who had quickly whipped his team into shape after an embarrassing Week 1. Actually you don’t really have to feel for Jeffrey. Up to you. But regardless, after a dominant performance, he somehow finds himself as the only 0-2 team in the league, and any further losses might creating too large of a deficit to overcome. If it’s any consolation, it does appear that operation #TankForSamuel was successful - he looks to be a reliable fantasy starter moving forward.

Miscellaneous Notes

Not sure where Jeffrey’s arrogance in the matchup against Kishan comes from - he’s historically 1-2 against the man, and with this week’s loss, he now slips to 1-3 in their head-to-head matchups. Not a good look at all.

For Kishan, this is not only the first time he’s cleared 130 points in over a year (his last time clearing that threshold was in 2020 Week 13, when he pummeled my team and eliminated me from the playoffs), but this is the first time in his career (well, at least the past 3 years) that he’s been the top scoring team in the league. Sensational grit from the man, perhaps his offseason confidence wasn’t simply all talk.

Jeffrey’s no stranger to slow starts to the season. Last year, it took him till Week 4 to clear 110 points for the first time, but he finished the year going over 110 8 out of the last 10 weeks of the regular season en route to a championship finish. Let’s see if he can turn on the jets again this year.

It’s already becoming abundantly clear that there’s massively more parity this year than in years past, with 10 teams sitting at 1-1 and only 44 points separating the top scoring and lowest scoring teams on the year. This sets up for a thrilling divisional and playoff race.

Plaxico Burress’s Handguns
1. Next Year (Ishan) [1-1]

Power rank: 1 | Panic level: 1/10

Despite whatever Ishan would like you to believe, he’s well set up to go on a deep run this year. Swift / Fournette / Diggs is shaping up to be probably the best 3-man group in the league, and Wentz / Sutton have been rock-solid contributors so far.

The Schultz injury is annoying but by no means takes away from what has been a very encouraging start to the year.

2. 2 Girls, 1 Kupp (Akshay) [1-1]

Power rank: 3 | Panic level: 2/10

While Ishan’s team is built a bit more top-heavy, Akshay’s team is led by one bona-fide superstar in Cooper Kupp (who’s on pace to have an even better year than last year) along with several other real solid pieces (Najee, Gibson, Mike Williams, Pat Friermuth, etc.)

I definitely have a personal preference towards top-heavy builds, but Akshay’s got to like where his team is at.

3. Temple of Amon-Ra (Methis) [1-1]

Power rank: 6 | Panic level: 6/10

Well for starters I couldn’t be happier to have Jettas, Saquads, and Sun God on my team. Sure Saquads and Jettas had relative down weeks, but I know they’ll be fine long term.

But that’s about where the fun ends. We just lost Lance, our huge QB dart throw, to a season-long injury, and it’s appearing we may be in the process of losing Pitts to Arthur Smith’s headassery.

Definitely a scary position to be in with two critical roster positions in flux, but I think we’ve got enough pieces to build around.

4. P. Sherman Wallerby Way (Madurais) [1-1]

Power rank: 9 | Panic level: 7/10

With Dalvin Cook looking washed and CeeDee Lamb stuck with Cooper Rush, it’s looking like the two biggest bets that the Madurais had to have superstars on their team are going to be massive letdowns on the year.

That’s putting massive pressure on Mahomes and Waller to be top-tier options at their respective positions to give this team any chance at sustainably winning games. That’s a lot to gamble on.

Obviously it’s early on in the season, but the Madurais may need to quickly pivot and do some roster reshuffling to avoid falling behind in the league.

Tom Brady’s Deflated Balls
1. Scboom5 (Shivang) [1-1]

Power rank: 4 | Panic level: 2/10

Kelce and Lamar look like solid early-season bets to finish at the tops of their respective positions, Pittman looks like he’s ready to take a year 3 leap, and even though Ekeler looks exceedingly unlikely to repeat his 20-TD performance of last year, he looks like he’ll be a consistent option and finish in the top-10 RBs.

There’s a lot to like in his early season roster build, and even though his depth is highly suspect, his star-studded lineup should give him a chance to win every week regardless of his opponnet.

2. Booty Shorts Babu (Varun) [1-1]

Power rank: 2 | Panic level: 5/10

Despite how strong his WR trio of Adams, Samuel, and Thomas looks, massive alarm bells should be ringing around his QB and top 2 RBs (Russel, Javonte, and Zeke). If the Broncos are able to turn it around, this team is probably looking mighty fine, but I definitely would not want to be in a position where I’m highly leveraged on two players dependent on Nathaniel Hackett.

3. The Mixon Administration (Sitarams) [1-1]

Power rank: 8 | Panic level: 8/10

While not as bad as being highly leveraged on the Broncos, the Sitarams high-amount of leverage on the Bengals (with their Burrow / Mixon stack) looks pretty suspect itself as the Bengals look like an absolute shell of their previous Super Bowl caliber selves.

And with the injury count rising on their team, it’s looking like Aaron Jones might be their only shot at having a top-5 player at their respective position, although I’d bet on Jones being a highly volatile option moving forward.

I dunno, just seems like there’s lots of question marks swirling around on this roster.

4. Born Again Christian (Anant) [1-1]

Power rank: 11 | Panic level: 7/10

I’d say the panic has cooled off a little bit since last week. While Etienne / McLaurin still look like relative busts at their cost, Hurts looks like he’s worth every penny that Anant paid for him and more, and McCaffrey’s usage in this past game is at least a little encouraging (although it’s still a violation that he only got 5 targets).

Even so, it seems like the recipe for this team is to pray that Hurts and CMC combine for 50 and hope that the rest of the roster can scrap together 70 points. Curious to see how this strategy plays out for the rest of the year.

Michael Vick’s Dog Pound
1. Ja’Marr Chase & Co. (Anish) [2-0]

Power rank: 12 | Panic level: 6/10

Anish has to be thanking his lucky stars that he’s 2-0 up to this point in the season given that he’s got the lowest points scored so far, giving him a small cushion that he frankly does not deserve.

In any case, it does look like he’s got the pieces he needs to put on much stronger performances over the course of the season. Tyreek and Kyler look like relative hits at their draft cost, and if Kamara and Chase can hit, that’s gotta be one of the strongest 4-man groups in the league. That’s a lot of ifs though, and the depth behind those players does not look encouraging in the slightest.

2. Mr. Ugg Boots (Aman) [1-1]

Power rank: 5 | Panic level: 3/10

This seems like a fluky week. Evans got ejected, Taylor only got 9 carries (absolutely criminal), and AJ Brown was the victim of the Eagles not having to pass in the 2nd half.

Better weeks should be ahead for this Josh-Allen led team. It’s ceiling is as high as any.

3. plumsmuggler99 (Kishan) [1-1]

Power rank: 7 | Panic level: 6/10

Oh how encouraging it is to see Kishan have a contending team up to this point in the year.

Kishan’s Chubb / Hunt RB duo looks devastating, and Tee Higgins and Goedert look like they’ll be real solid all year long. He’s got Christian Kirk ready to slot in over DJ Moore (who’s probably a bust). This lineup is not bad at all.

The biggest concern is with Derrick Henry who looks like he’s lost a step and is stuck on a god awful Titans team that can’t move the ball. If Henry does prove to be a bust, then all of Kishan’s hopes essentially ride on his Browns RB duo. Risky business.

4. Chink Town Bois 2.0 (Jeffrey) [0-2]

Power rank: 10 | Panic level: 7/10

Waddle / Mark Andrews look like massive hits, and Curtis Samuel is probably going to be a contender for waiver wire pickup of the year. Even though Herbert probably won’t live up to his cost, he should be a solid option all year long.

But Jeffrey’s RBs definitely look mad sus. Conner is hurt, Montgomery is on the Bears, and Akers just looks sus overall.

Looks like some more retooling is in store for this team, but he doesn’t have much time as he’s the only 0-2 team in the league.
