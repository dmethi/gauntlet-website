This week saw some stunning highs as well as crushing blows. Even though we saw some of the highest scoring weeks in league history, we’ve already seen some of the league’s top players go down with lengthy injuries.

Let’s get to it.

Kishan (1-1) vs Methis (1-1)
Thankfully, my team bounced back in a big way after a tragic week 1 loss. Despite being the “worst manager” of the week according to Sleeper (smh), my team put up a healthy 134.34 points, led by Kyler Murray (QB4 on the year) and Calvin Ridley (WR1 on the year). Also, despite having 4 RBs score over 18 points, I chose to start the one who ended up with 0.9 points on the week. You win some, you lose some. Sigh. With Miles Sanders and James Conner back from injury, Jonathan Taylor looking like a workhorse back, and a recent acquisition of Adam Thielen, my team looks like it’s on the rise heading into my bye week against Akshay next week (a matchup in which Sleeper already gives me an 83% chance of winning).

In any case, if this week is any indication, the Sitarams will be able to withstand this devastating blow as Melvin Gordon, Julian Edelman, Josh Allen, and Hayden Hurst look like solid sleeper picks. They’ll look to carry this week’s momentum and run their devastating WR trio into next week’s showdown with “I’m putting my entire team on the trading block” Shivang.

Varun (0-2) vs Anant (2-0)
This was an absolutely record-setting matchup, and frankly, it’s tragic that one of these teams had to lose. This was the 3rd highest scoring matchup in league history (including last year), and Varun set the record for the highest score in a losing effort (which, ironically, was held by Anant from week 3 last year where he put up 137 in a losing effort against yours truly). Varun lost while scoring 143 points, while Jeffrey won with only 109 points. You really hate to see it.

Let’s get to Anant’s team, which just happened to post the 4th-highest score in league history. Including last week’s performance, Anant now has the 3rd highest 2-week total from the past two years. What a thunderous start to the season. He came out of Thursday’s matchup down big, but rebounded in a MASSIVE way led by Cam Newton and Aaron Jones who combined for nearly 80 points (Aaron Jones alone nearly outscored Aman’s team). He only had one player score less than 12 points, and that happened to be Will Fuller who put up a donut. Cam Newton and Noah Fant look to be sensational sleeper picks (I think Anant paid a total of $3 between the two of them) as they’re both 3rd overall at their respective positions. His momentum is at risk of coming to a screeching halt, as McCaffrey looks to be out 4-6 weeks with an ankle sprain. He'll be starting Zach Moss (yikes) in next week’s matchup against Aman.

In any case, he’s not poorly situated as he heads into a week 3 date versus the Sitarams in what should be another high scoring affair.

Aman (1-1) vs Jeffrey (1-1)
What a shitshow of a matchup. With 164 points scored, this was the 8th lowest-scoring matchup of the last 2 years. Anant himself outscored both of these teams combined. Even though this was one of the lowest scoring matchups in league history, this was one of the biggest blowouts as Jeffrey more than doubled Aman’s score - which happened to be only the 3rd time in history a team has failed to crack 60 points. What’s even more shocking is that this comes on the heels of Aman’s 147 point league-leading effort in Week 1. This is only the second time someone has had a 90 point drop from one week to the next - Varun went from 166 to 83 last year. But Varun did rebound with a 181 point performance, so who knows Aman could be back on top next week.

In any case, Jeffrey miraculously pulled a victory despite scoring 10 points below league average. This marks the 11th week in a row that Jeffrey has failed to cross 115 points. This pedestrian effort was still sufficient in this matchup, as Darren Waller and Matt Ryan combined nearly outscored Aman. My guess is that brighter weeks lie ahead for Jeffrey. His RBs had a down week, he’s got James Robinson on his bench, and his K/DEF combined for only 5 points which is due for regression. Maybe he’ll finally break the elusive 115 point benchmark next week against Varun.

League notes


This was a super high scoring week overall. We had the second highest weekly average score in the past 2 years. There were only 25 instances in which a team scored over 130 points, yet that happened 5 times this week.

QB scoring is off the charts this year - there was never a week in our league where the average starting QB scored 21 points, but that’s already happened both weeks. This week, 6 different QB starters scored more than 27 points - that’s insane.

It’s always fun to see how everyone’s faring in special teams (defense + kicker) since, in my opinion, it’s mostly luck. This year, Shivang is leading the way with an average of 28 points per week from his kicker and defense - that’s 8 points higher than anyone else. On the other hand, the Madurais are only averaging 8.5 points per week from their kicker and defense. That’s a 20 point swing right there between the two teams.
