Ishan WR - He’s getting 9.1 points per week at that position over league average, getting 37.2 points per week from his top two wideouts.

Kishan RB - He’s getting 9.1 points per week at that position over league average, getting 37.1 points per week from his top two running backs.

Not saying this is predictive, but so far into the year, it’s clear that stacking positional advantages and paying up for studs has been a very successful strategy for these teams.

Somewhat surprisingly this year, QB has a massive differentiator — it is 3 of the 6 largest positional advantages, and 3 of the top 7 largest positional disadvantages. Let’s see if this trend continues, because it flies in the face of the prevailing logic that says it’s not worth it to invest in a top QB. The teams with Hurts, Allen, and Lamar have a massive early-season advantage over the rest of the league.
