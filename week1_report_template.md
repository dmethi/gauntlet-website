Gauntlet NFC — Week 1
League Overview (Assistant’s Notes)

Scoring in the NFC felt uneven. A full seven teams cleared 110, but most lineups under-shot projections, and touchdowns came in bunches instead of balance. No take-backs after Week 1—but let’s not crown or bury anyone yet. Also, some rosters still don’t have team names. Rename your teams or I’ll do it for you.

vayyala (94.9) vs lurski (134.7) — lurski wins

Thursday gave Vinny false hope with Hurts, but the rest of his roster folded fast. His defense (Broncos) finished as his second-highest scorer, which tells you all you need to know. Lurski, meanwhile, spread production across the lineup, getting big bumps from multiple positions and making this the most lopsided game in the NFC.

Odds & Ends
Lurski’s 134.7 was the NFC high for Week 1.
Broncos D/ST propped up Vinny—one of the rare teams where D/ST was a top scorer.

joshrubatFD (82.4) vs Bego60 (111.4) — Bego60 wins

Jeffrey’s team fell on its face Thursday (AJB, Ferguson, and Day all duds) and never recovered. Henry and Bijan tried to salvage it, but Lamar on Bego’s side closed the door emphatically. Bego didn’t need brilliance elsewhere; steady double-digits across the lineup were enough.

Odds & Ends
Jeffrey started three RBs; they carried most of his total despite the Thursday faceplant.
Turnovers hurt both sides, but Lamar papered over everything for Bego.

RithikP (100.2) vs cescott25 (111.4) — cescott25 wins

Varun’s squad was balanced and nearly stole this one, but Burrow’s implosion at QB left too big a hole. Christian leaned on Josh Allen, who detonated on Sunday night and flipped the matchup. Christian’s WRs were invisible (34 yards combined!), but Allen’s line erased all sins.

Odds & Ends
Varun’s WR room was targeted plenty but didn’t cash in. Efficiency was rough.
Etienne + Breece posted the top rushing yardage by a duo this week, in a losing effort.

DJ Herbussy (104.9) vs YouSmellLikeDaal (101.7) — Herbussy wins

Best game of the week. Mahomes vs Herbert lit up Friday, Ollie Gordon was the bold but costly start for Aman, and Monday came down to Jefferson vs DJ Moore + Swift. Herbussy eked it out with Jefferson falling just short. And to rub it in, Herbussy left two 20+ scorers (Coleman and Egbuka) rotting on the bench.

Odds & Ends
Herbussy’s backfield handled a workhorse workload.
Aman’s Ollie Gordon gamble cost him—safer bench WRs both cleared 20.

ziyanp22 (111.7) vs arnavmehta (101.8) — ziyanp22 wins

Tight game through Sunday, but Monday sealed it: Minnesota D/ST plus Rome Odunze pushed Ziyan over the line. The highlight was the WR duel — Chase vs Puka — where Puka outclassed Ja’Marr in every way.

Odds & Ends
Both sides leaned heavy on their RBs. Big volume, modest yardage.
Ziyan’s backs epitomized the grind: 40 carries for 81 yards.

dmethi (73.1) vs lukebowsh (113.2) — lukebowsh wins

This was over early. Pickens and Worthy gave Dhruv nothing, and the rest of the roster barely made it halfway to projections. Luke, on the other hand, got steady production from Baker, CMC, Lamb, and Deebo. Comfortable win, no sweat.

Odds & Ends
Tough sledding: no starters beat projection, and only three players hit 10+.

Gauntlet AFC — Week 1
League Overview (Assistant’s Notes)

The AFC didn’t exactly light it up either — lots of teams finished well below projection. The difference? More chaos. We had bench explosions, QB craters, and a cousin showdown that flipped back and forth like a bad soap opera. In other words: the AFC is already messy, which feels about right.

Nacua Matata (102.3) vs To Infinity and Bijan (120.4) — Infinity wins

Coworker clash: staff engineer vs EM. Joel jumped ahead on Friday with Mahomes/Kelce/Ladd, then Bijan’s long TD slammed the door. EM’s Puka and Jayden flashed but never made it close. Joel even benched his defense Monday like he was playing with house money. (Bookmark this if tiebreakers get ugly.)

Odds & Ends

Both sides wasted firepower: Joel stranded two 20+ guys, EM benched Zay Flowers. That tied for most in the AFC.

After Bijan’s score, win probability didn’t budge again.

benweinfeld (85.2) vs Dr Patel Parikh MD MBA (119.5) — Dr Patel wins

Straightforward. Dr Patel’s lineup clicked around Lamar, while Ben’s was one long sigh. His rookie WR flashed, but not nearly enough.

Odds & Ends

Ben’s bench scored the most in the AFC. Enough to keep it respectable, not enough to change the outcome.

QB gap was among the largest of the week — Lamar did work.

The Golden Age (115.8) vs vchak (67.3) — Golden Age wins

Beatdown. Caleb did enough, Henry trucked, and Golden Age coasted even though Gibbs and Ja’Marr had quiet weeks. Vchak only got life from CMC and Ricky Pearsall; the rest were duds, and the bench offered nothing.

Odds & Ends

Golden Age only needed three starters to outscore vchak’s entire roster — lowest “fewest-players” number in the AFC.

vchak led the AFC in sub-5 scorers. Hard to win when half your lineup ghosts.

lol jerry jones (91.3) vs NielGetsCarried (89.5) — lol jerry wins

The cousin derby. Neil looked buried Thursday, rebounded Sunday on Fields + Packers D, then fell short Monday with Swift + Jefferson just missing the needed 24. Painful stuff.

Odds & Ends

The defensive swing was huge: Packers vs Lions was the second-largest D/ST gap in the AFC this week.

The game had 12 win-prob flips — second-most in the AFC.

Quonspiracy Theorists (98.3) vs scboom5 (105.0) — scboom wins

This one seesawed all weekend: Hurts/Lamb gave QT the start, Burrow cratered it, Saquon/Deebo pulled it back, then Khalil Shakir slammed the door. Classic Week 1 drama.

Odds & Ends

This was the most chaotic AFC game: 14 win-prob flips and the single biggest one-play swing of the week.

Burrow’s dud gave scboom one of the largest QB advantages in the slate.

2 Dolla Balla$ (115.8) vs achak7 (103.4) — 2 Dolla wins

Josh Allen nuked the matchup with one play; Dak never answered. Achak’s Broncos D was a massive edge, but it wasn’t enough.

Achak actually had the largest defensive edge in the AFC, and still lost. That’s how lopsided QB was this week.
